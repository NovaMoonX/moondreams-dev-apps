# Forms and CRUD conventions

Part of the repo conventions split out of `CLAUDE.md` (see its "Read before you touch" table). Rules here bind exactly as if they were in `CLAUDE.md`; a reference like `CLAUDE.md "X"` in older text means the bullet titled X, now in whichever conventions file holds it.

- **Form and section titles are plain nouns**, whatever the container (modal, drawer or subview): "Expense", "Visit", "New cat", never CRUD verbs ("Add Expense", "Edit Visit"). The same title serves create and edit. The verb belongs on the primary action button and on a destructive confirm dialog's title.
- **Submit buttons disable until required fields are valid** (`onDataChange` + `isValid`); no required-field asterisks. When a row is what blocks the submit, the pinned footer says what to fix as a link to that row, which scrolls to it and focuses it.
- **Truly optional extras use a "+ Add X" reveal link** (or a chip, see `AddFieldChips`), not "(optional)" in the label. **On a form of summary rows (a `QuestionFlow` once reviewed, an edit), an added extra becomes a row like the others, never a bare input (owner, Runway cards round 4):** `@/components/forms/OptionalDetailRows` draws the added ones as `SummaryRow`s with a chevron under one product-worded heading ("More about it"), a row opens its field in a sheet whose footer holds a small tertiary Remove on the left, level with Done, and Done on an empty field takes it back off; the Add chips stay for the extras not yet added, and an Add chip never turns into a Remove chip. Waypoint's `RemovableField` forms predate this and move onto it in their own PR. Keep the "+" for short labels (a verb and a noun or two); a sentence-like label ("Can't find it? Add it by title") gets no plus. A detail the user's path depends on is not an optional extra: it is asked ("A journey detail is asked, not tucked away", under Design & UX). An action beside a section title is vertically centered on the title line (`SectionHeader` does this). **Fields a record normally has are not extras:** a recipe's servings, times, photo, description and steps are visible sections of its form, never chips at the bottom.
- **An always-mounted form container (a modal, drawer or subview shown via `isOpen`) needs `key={editingX?.id ?? 'new'}`** at its call site, or its internal state goes stale across edit targets. One that's conditionally rendered should be keyed on whatever identity it depends on.
- **Deletes go through `useActionModal().confirm(...)` with `destructive: true`** — never a bare `window.confirm` or no confirmation. **An act that is undone for a few seconds asks no confirm:** taking one line off a shopping list, clearing what you've bought or tossing a box raises an Undo toast instead (guarded against a double tap); a whole record keeps its confirm, and a confirm names everything that goes with it (a recipe's cooks and notes), offering to hide instead when hiding keeps something the person made.
- **A form's delete is an icon-only trash button, bottom-left of the footer**, in a modal or in a subview's pinned footer alike. Use the shared `DeleteIconButton` as `leftActions` of `ModalFooterActions` (`rightActions` holds Cancel then the primary submit). Never a "Delete" text button in the Cancel/Save row. References: `ChecklistItemFormModal.tsx`, `ExpenseFormModal.tsx`.
- **Dreamer UI's `Form` reads `initialData` once.** To change its values from code, remount it with a key that changes on every change (a counter, so re-picking the same item still remounts). **A change form's preview shows the effect of what the person changed, never of the form's default:** seed the form from what is in force on the day the change would apply, and draw nothing until a field differs (otherwise opening a form over a scheduled raise previews cancelling it).
- **`SummaryEditor` focuses its field when it opens, and a date or number field has no text selection to set:** focus it, never call `setSelectionRange` on it (it throws on those input types).
- **An add step confirms itself and never loses typing.** A form clears its field only after the write succeeds (a failed save keeps what was typed), and a successful add either returns to the list, where the new row is the confirmation, or says what was added; never clear the field and stay silent. A cap or uniqueness check that lives only in the UI is stated as such in `TECHNICAL.md`.
- **A form or add opened on behalf of another record says so.** Show "For {event}" while it applies a link, prefill what the context knows, never let a field (private) silently detach it, and toast what was added and to what when the sheet it came from closes. A form opened from a drawer returns to it, showing the result (the drawer is the hub), and a new record's add lands on its own drawer, whose primary is the next step. A row of corner tabs is checked at 320px at 100% and with slightly larger text: on a phone each tab is the emoji plus one short word (full phrase in `aria-label`), and an emphasized state is a soft tint plus a visible border, never quieter than its neutral sibling.
- **A settings form that fills a page (not a sheet) pins its status and Save row to the bottom of the screen** (`sticky bottom-0` at the end of the form, `bg-background/90` with `backdrop-blur`), because its fields can run past a phone screen and "unsaved changes" must stay in sight.
- **A form's footer must be able to pin.** `ModalFooterActions` is a direct child of the form grid (a wrapper around it is `contents`), or its `sticky` has no room to move; check that Cancel and the primary action are on screen the moment a sheet opens at 390×844. A Dreamer UI `Select` filters its own list: never mirror its search into state to filter or cap the options yourself (`onSearch` doesn't fire when it resets).
- **Fields inside a `Modal` use `variant='outline'`:** `solid` is the card's own color in dark mode and the field disappears. A modal step whose list grows puts its input above the list and caps the list's height, so the field and footer never fall below the fold at 390×844. **An open dropdown is never clipped by the sheet it sits in:** a `Select`'s list in a modal or drawer goes past the card (portaled or unclipped) and stays inside the viewport, and a modal that only fits by scrolling around its own open list is the wrong height (owner, Runway round 4: the twice-a-month day pickers' list was cut inside the Income source modal at 1280). A height cap on a `Select`'s list (`dropdownClassName`) caps its inner scroller too, or rows paint below the box (round 5: a `max-h-48!` list kept a 240px scroller).
- **One scroll area per surface: a fixed header, and only the list scrolls beneath it** (a column cap never sits below its own header plus the list's floor, or the sheet stops short and the list ends up under the footer; under about 500px of height the sheet body is the one scroller, and a filter chip with its own ✕ gets no Clear beside it: from two chips a quiet "Clear all" chip, never a link). The text above a list and its search field sit in a `shrink-0` header, never overlaid on the list (no `sticky` over rows: a drawer body's own padding lets rows slide through the gap above it); the list is the one scroller below. In a `DetailSheet`/`Drawer` that means a column `flex max-h-[max(12rem,calc(92dvh-22rem))] flex-col` (the `22rem` leaves room for the drawer's title and footer) with a `min-h-0 flex-1 overflow-y-auto` list, so the drawer's own body never scrolls. Content shared between a drawer (phones) and a popover (desktop) applies its cap on the popover side only. Check it with the sheet open: the drawer body's `scrollHeight` equals its `clientHeight`, exactly one descendant scrolls, and scrolling the list never moves the header. **A capped list keeps a floor of about three rows** (`[@media(max-height:800px)]:min-h-36`) and its cap counts every line of the header above it: measure the list's `clientHeight` at 375×667 and 320×568, where it is never 0 (the drawer body may scroll a little there, the accepted fallback, rather than a list nobody can see). A step view swapped into a sheet (a lines view with a total to hold in sight) keeps its fixed question, figure and search in a `shrink-0` header and scrolls only its list, with the same floor of about three rows (Runway's confirm Lines view).
- **A search's results inside a `Modal` are capped (a `max-h` with scroll, about four rows) and measured at 320px with the most the API can return,** so the field and footer never move or fall off the screen as results arrive; Enter in such a field never submits the form around it. A picker hosted in a sheet takes its cap from the shared picker (`ItemSearch` `isCapped`), never one of its own per host (round 8: two hosts missed it, and their whole drawer scrolled after typing at 667px). A range's two boxes keep a visible caption after they are filled in (a placeholder is not a label).
- **Pending-request/invite features ship both sides together**: owner approve/decline *and* requester cancel/withdraw (delete their own doc, destructive confirm).
- **A row action that creates or removes a record has an in-flight guard.** Its row is disabled until the write settles (a planned meal's "Mark as cooked", the same row in the recipe drawer's ⋯ More), so a second tap during the acknowledgement lag can't make a second record and a second Undo toast. A second deliberate action after the toast is fine. A row removed from a list the person is tapping through stays in place, struck through and saying what happened ("Taken off your list"), until the sheet closes, so the next tap never lands on the row that slid up.
- **Static option lists** (dropdown options, role/status allowlists) are declared once in the app's `constants.ts` (never `types.ts`, which is types only) and imported everywhere. Reference: `src/apps/waypoint/constants.ts`.
