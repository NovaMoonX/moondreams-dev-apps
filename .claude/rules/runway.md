# Runway rules

Applies on top of CLAUDE.md to everything under `src/apps/runway/`. Keep `.github/instructions/runway.instructions.md` identical to this body.

- **Tone: plain and matter-of-fact, an exception to "Playful by default".** Runway speaks like a calm advisor: it reports what happened and what it affects ("Dining is $18 over. It would take $18 from Everyday spending.") and stays considerate, never curt, cold or blaming. No scoring, streaks, scolding or cheerleading, and no cute emoji: an emoji appears where the person chose one for an envelope, bill or goal, on a quick pick that sets one (a suggested bill or envelope), and as the fixed mark of a Plan view (💵 Income, 🧾 Bills, ✉️ Envelopes, 🎯 Goals, dropped below 360px); never in copy, toasts or warnings. Empty states use a lucide icon in `EmptyCard`, not an emoji.
- **Don't make me think (or read) too much.** Runway handles more money detail than its screens should show at once, so every screen is judged by how little a person has to read and decide before they can act (TurboTax is the model: one question at a time in large type, a running summary, the figure that matters always the biggest thing). Six rules follow from it, and a screen that breaks one is a finding:
  - **The amount is the hero.** Any money entry (a purchase, a bill, an envelope, a goal, income, Confirm money arrived, a change of amount) opens on a large centred figure (`@/components/AmountHero`, about 48px, tabular, focused on open on a phone), never an amount box the size of the other fields. A prefilled amount the person usually just accepts (Confirm money arrived) stays unfocused, so the keyboard never hides the button they came to press. The figure reads grouped ("2,140.00") whenever it is not being typed in, and tabbing in and typing replaces it. The ways to change what it is ("It varies", which splits it into two halves "$100 to $140"; "This is a refund"; "A % of pay"; "Whatever is left") are `RevealChip`s under the figure, at most two visible and the rest behind one "More ways" chip, never a pill row above the amount.
  - **One question at a time; answered ones fold.** A create form is a `@/components/QuestionFlow`: the next question in `text-2xl` with only its control, every answered question above it as a one-line summary row that reopens it, and nothing below it but the answers still to come, folded, when an earlier one is reopened. Next is the pinned footer's primary while a question is open and becomes Add only at the review; a Next that is off says why in the footer note beside it. A default that is nearly always right (priority Should, today, every income day, from now on) is pre-answered and shown folded, never asked, but a fact only the person knows (a bill's due date) is never preselected. Escape, back, Cancel and the close button ask Keep editing / Discard once the person has typed an answer. A question whose answer is one tap (an envelope pill, a repeat once its date is in) moves on by itself, so the most frequent entry never gains a Next; a field a chip reveals takes focus. The last step is the folded rows and one primary. Check the primary at 844×390 and 320×568 on the longest flow's last question.
  - **A row is a glance: name, one line, the figure.** Every list row (income, bills, envelopes, goals, a draft's lines, purchases, income days) is the item badge, the name, at most one muted line with the single most useful fact ("Due Nov 1", "$350 of $600", "Every income day"), the amount on the right and a chevron. No chips, no priority pill, no second detail line. The one line is never cut: it runs under the figure, clamps at two lines (never `truncate`) and is written short ("Due Nov 1", short month names), measured at 320px with the longest real fact. The row opens the item's sheet (`ItemSheet`): its head is the figure and one fact line (a change ahead is its own row, notes behind a `HelpTip`), its first action is the most frequent change opened at that question ("Change amount"), an action row has a subtitle only when it says a consequence, and it does not scroll at 390×844. Moving an action off a row counts its taps before and after for each moment that uses it, and the moment that needs it most keeps it at one tap: ticking a bill on the payday checklist records it paid, with an Undo. A tick that writes shows at once and comes back with a toast that says why when the write fails (marking paid needs a connection). A bulk tick ("Mark all paid") writes in transactions that re-read each record, reports the real count, has one Undo that reverses only what it wrote, and never un-does a record made elsewhere: once all is done it is a state, not a control.
  - **A shortfall is "$X short" everywhere** (a row, a sheet, a footer note, Home, Days), and "held" names only money that is actually held.
  - **A count names the first thing it counts.** A row that replaces sentences with a count says the soonest one in the plan's words and counts the rest ("The plan leaves Rent $30 short on Nov 1 · 4 more"); a note that needs no action is not counted.
  - **Read nothing you didn't ask for.** A caveat, an explanation or how a figure was worked out sits behind an info icon (`HelpTip`) beside the figure it qualifies, or in the item's sheet; a field's hint is one short line or none, and no sheet body holds a paragraph over two lines.
  - **The biggest number is the one you came for:** Free to spend on Home, the pay on an income day, saved-of-target on a goal, the amount on a form. Everything else is 14 to 16px.
  - **Budgets, measured at 390×844:** a tab front's first list row starts in the top 40% of the screen with at most 25 words above it; a row is at most two lines of text (three when its name wraps); a form shows one open question and at most four controls besides its footer on open. Print the counts in the PR.
- **A call to action is a verb and a noun, with no "+".** Runway's buttons, header actions and reveal chips read like a bank's or TurboTax's ("Add a bill", "Add expected money", "Add a place"), never "+ Expected" or "+ Add": the words carry the action, so the glyph adds nothing. A screen's one add action names what it makes.
- **A warning looks like one.** Anything that says the plan falls short (the problems row on Home and Days, a short line, a day that leaves a bill short) uses the warning tokens (`warning` tint, a warning-coloured icon chip, readable text in light and dark), never the app's blue, which reads as information. One warning surface per screen; the rows it opens keep the colour on their icon only.
- **Amounts are private until the person shows them.** Every money figure and every line that gives one away ("$30 short", a goal's saved amount) on a tab's front is blurred by default (`@/components/SensitiveValue`), and each tab's header has one eye button on its title line that shows or hides them for every tab at once. The choice is kept on this device only, like a bank app, and hides itself again when the tab has been in the background for five minutes. Forms, sheets the person opened on purpose and the figure they are typing are never blurred.
- **"See all" sits on its section's header line; "Show more" is a quiet row under the list.** A list that leads to a fuller screen puts a small "See all ›" right-aligned on its header's title line, as a bank app does; a list that unfolds in place ends in one compact muted row. Neither is larger than the rows' own detail text on a phone.
- **An action row is compact.** The rows of an item's sheet ("Change amount", "Mark paid", "History") use a 15px title and a 13px subtitle on a phone and 15/13 from `sm` (`ActionRow` without `isRoomy`), so four rows fit above the fold at 390×844 with the figure.
- **Theme: restrained blue.** `runway.css` sets the tokens on `html.runway-theme` (cool near-white surfaces, a deep slate-blue primary, a pale blue tint for selections), switched on by `useRunwayTheme()` while the app is mounted. Components use semantic tokens (`bg-primary`, `bg-accent`), never raw palette colors. Body and row text is the normal text color; blue is for filled buttons and real links. Check a filled button, a selected pill, a badge and a highlighted card in light and dark after any token change.
- **The level decides the wording.** An envelope figure reads *assigned* at Plan, *left* at Track and *safe to spend* at Link, never one word for all three. **Hidden, not disabled:** what depends on a level the person is not using (a Spend tab, Add purchase, Needs a home, Check an amount) is not on screen at all; `LevelGate` draws its children only at the level that needs them. Data for a hidden level is still listened to.
- **Over is shown, never blocked.** At Track an envelope that a purchase takes below zero reads "−$18.00 over" (`describeEnvelopeFigure`), Free to spend falls by exactly that overspend (an envelope total counts an over envelope as zero), and the form says so before saving in plain words. A purchase's "Add" is disabled only for what cannot be saved (no amount, no envelope, a day outside tracking), never for spending too much.
- **A range is always labelled** wherever it drives a figure ("planned at the low end, $140"), through the one `RangeNote` component.
- **Money is integer cents, dates are date-only UTC midnight.** Format money with `formatCents` and `MoneyInput`; display a date-only value with `formatDateUTC`, write it with `fromDateInputValue`. Schedules use `getUTC*` math and "today" is `todayAsUtcDate()`.
- **Sealed documents.** A document has a few readable fields (ids, dates, enums, flags, `createdAt`) and one `sealed` payload for every sensitive value. A readable field never holds a name, an amount, a merchant or a note. Only `security.ts` seals and opens; the data key lives in the query cache and `security.ts` closures, never in Redux or storage.
- **Rules are the beta tier: owner only, and history is append-only.** Everything under `apps/runway/plans/{uid}` is the owner's alone; `versions`, `changes`, `moves` and a day's records are create-only, so a write over an existing one is refused rather than overwriting (moves and records build their ids from their own fields, so a repeat fails on the id). The rules check no shape (sealed envelope, types, caps, write-once fields, a day's moves): the app's actions keep them. A new collection the app edits or deletes in joins the `update`/`delete` lists, and `npm run check:runway-rules` (its `COLLECTIONS` table) says what each one allows.
- **The engine is pure.** Everything under `engine/` takes values and returns values and imports nothing from React, the store or Firebase. The draft for an income day is computed, never stored or edited in place; the same inputs always give the same lines. Every engine change adds cases to `npm run check:runway-engine`.
- **Phone first, nothing disabled to explain.** Home, Days and Plan are the only tabs; every other section is a `Subview` or sheet reached from them. A figure says what it counts and what it leaves out, and a gap in the plan is never shown as a gap in the person's finances.
- **Every write dated in a closed month marks its closing stale.** A closing caches what the ledger summed to at a month's end, so a write that adds, changes or removes a day (confirm, correction), a bill paid or undone, a purchase or the starting day reads the plan in the same transaction and writes `dirtyFrom` through `getLedgerMark`/`markLedgerWrite` (`store/actions/closingActions.ts`); a new action or server pass that writes one of these does the same, and a new figure the balances read from the ledger is added to `closing()` in `engine/closings.ts` and to the equivalence check in `check:runway-engine`. Spending stays apart in a closing so the level can change. While closings are rebuilt the app draws "Updating your history" and nothing else, never a figure from before the change.
- **Scale.** Lists of envelopes, bills, goals and purchases are checked at 5 and at 50. `scripts/seeds/runwayScale.ts` is the oversized fixture (`npm run seed:runway-scale`, then sign in as Morgan: five years of Track with 25,000 purchases and no closings, so the first open builds them; `RUNWAY_SCALE_CLOSINGS=1` seeds them too); extend it whenever a list is added, and judge performance on it per CLAUDE.md. A long list of rows mounts in chunks of 12 under `LazyMount`, and the lookahead, the one figure that walks a year of income days, is drawn after the screen has painted (`useDeferredLookahead`), so no figure holds up Home or Days.
- **An envelope's priority and emoji wear one badge each.** `PriorityBadge` is a soft tint (Must a primary tint, Should the accent, Want muted), never a solid fill; `ItemBadge` is the envelope's emoji, or the first letter of its name when it has none. A row pins both to its first-line band so a long name wraps without moving them.
- **Stopping an item is not erasing it.** An item's sheet (`ItemSheet`) pauses a goal, ends an item after a last day, or archives it; each shows its effect on the coming income days first and asks "Why?" once. Ending and archiving set a write-once header field and leave every version, record and confirmed day as it was. A change that lasts for a stretch is a version with `effectiveUntil`, and the draft says so on the first day the earlier amount is back ("Vacation fund returns to $300.00 this time").
- **A form's preview shows the effect of what the person changed, never of the form's starting values.** A change form seeded from the version in force draws "Change a field and its effect shows here" until a field differs (`useChangeFlow` sends no change while `isUnchanged`), so opening it never previews a move nobody made.
- **Every view an action leads to has one anatomy.** Pause, End, Archive, the change forms, Correct a day and Check an amount share one shape (`components/ActionSection`): a Back chip, the question in `text-2xl` (`ActionTitle`) with its control under it, then each further section as one muted `text-xs` caption (`ActionSection`, or the caption the shared `SuggestionChips` already draws) over its card or chips, and the reason last ("Why the change?" with its chips and one short note box). Never a centred divider, a left medium-weight line and a bare sentence in the same sheet. The effects card leads each row with the lines that change ("Vacation fund $300.00 → $0.00"), shows "Left from this day" only when it moves, folds days that move alike into one row ("6 income days go away"), shows three and "Show N more", and the whole view fits 390×844 collapsed. The primary is a verb and the item's noun ("End the goal"). A rename moves nothing, so it asks no "From when" and shows no effect section. What an item's sheet opens (History, Purchases, an action's view) swaps in place under a Back chip, and a form it opens returns to it on Cancel, Escape and the browser's back.
- **A date-only value never reaches sealed summary copy as ISO.** A change-log summary, a seed and a record say "Nov 15" through `formatDateUTC`, never `toISOString().slice(0, 10)`.
- **A warning about the plan is a door.** A row that says a bill runs short or has no income opens the income day or the bills it is about (`ProblemRow`), and it says "the plan leaves Rent short" rather than "Rent runs short", since the plan is what is short, not the person's money.
- **A word that names a figure names one figure on a screen.** "Free to spend" is the money on hand less what has a job (Home, its detail, Check an amount, a purchase); what one income day leaves after its lines is "Left from this day" (`DRAFT_WORDS.dayLeft`), so a day's figure never disagrees with Home's.
- **A step view inside a sheet keeps its context pinned.** The Back chip, the sentence, the figure it is about ("Left from this day $0.00 of $1,500.00") and the search stay fixed, and only the list under them scrolls (`max-h-[max(9rem,calc(92dvh-29rem))] overflow-y-auto`, the whole body scrolling under 500px of height), so a long list never pushes the figure a person is steering by off screen. Measure the list's `clientHeight` at 390×844, 320×568 and 1280×900.
- **A modal is capped to the screen.** `runway.css` caps a modal's card at `100dvh - 2rem` and lets it scroll inside itself with its footer pinned, so a long form is never taller than the window with its title cut off; do not edit the shared `FormSheet` for it.
- **A tab owns its scroll and its history.** Each tab opens where it was left (the top on a first visit), tapping the open tab goes to its top, and only leaving Home adds a history entry, so Back from Plan, Days or Spend returns to Home first (`useRunwayUrlState.showTab`, `RunwayTabs`). A subview closed by the back gesture gives the tab its scroll back after the browser's own restore.
- **A row carries no chips.** Change, More, Mark paid, Add purchase, Purchases and Check an amount live in the item's sheet, which the row opens; Home's Envelopes header offers Add purchase at Track, and an envelope opened from Home is its sheet.
- **A tick is measured on the control, not on its row.** A 20px checkbox gets a 40px hit area from `TICK_HIT_CLASS`; check `elementFromPoint` 19px from its centre on all four sides.
- **A first run shows only the way in.** Days draws no "+ Expected" and Plan draws no section pills until an income source exists; Home offers one "Continue setting up" card while the plan has income and nothing else, and it opens the Starting out steps at the first one with nothing in it.
