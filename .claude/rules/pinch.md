# Pinch rules

Applies on top of CLAUDE.md to everything under `src/apps/pinch/`. Keep the other copy of this file identical in body.

- **Theme: a warm kitchen, quietly.** Surfaces are paper-warm (cream by day, warm black by night) with hairline borders, and a soft shadow only where something floats (the nav pill, a popover, a sheet). Buttons are espresso-ink filled (`--color-primary`) for the one action on a screen and see-through with a hairline for the rest; never a block of orange. Warmth comes from peach tints (`bg-accent`, `--pinch-drizzle-soft`) on groups, fact chips, selected pills and the cards that need a nudge of attention, and from orange as the accent: the today dot (`--pinch-drizzle-fill` with `--pinch-drizzle-fill-foreground`, the only place text sits on the orange), a selected outline and ring, the active tab icon, the default product's star, the dashed "New" pills. A screen still reads clean: tint a few things, not everything. The palette, radii, hairlines and field inset live in `pinch.css` as tokens on `html.pinch-theme`, switched on by `usePinchTheme()` while the app is mounted so portaled modals and drawers match. Change the look there, not per component, use semantic tokens (`bg-primary`, `bg-accent`) and the drizzle variable (`text-[var(--pinch-drizzle)]`) rather than raw colors, and body and row text stays the normal color (the tertiary-button rule in `pinch.css`). The app icon and banner follow the other apps: a black tile or banner, the white mark and name, and "Moondreams Dev" in grey.
- **Shape language.** Corners are set by the raised `--radius-*` tokens, so don't hand-roll radii. Options a person picks among are `Pill`s, never radios or a tab strip; an option is a hairline pill and the selected one gets a drizzle outline so its emoji stays readable. Navigation is a floating pill at the bottom of a phone, as wide as its four tabs and never stretched across the screen (Calendar, Recipes, Kitchen, Shopping: `PinchNav`, each tab 64px wide at least and 10px of side padding below `sm` so all four fit 320px on one line), and the same tabs stand in a rail on the left edge from `lg` up. The Shopping tab shows the count left to buy as a small `bg-primary` badge on its icon (only above zero, "99+" past 99) read by its own tiny component, so a tick never re-renders the nav. A later feature joins the tab it belongs to (prepared meals and, later, ingredient stock to Kitchen) and is not a fifth tab.
- **Wide screens use the width, not a stretched phone.** The calendar's cells show meal emoji chips on a soft tint of the meal's colour below `lg` and, from `lg`, Google-Calendar-style pills with the meal's truncated title; Week is an agenda on a phone and seven columns from `lg`; Recipes, Kitchen and Shopping run in two columns from `lg`. The phone layout is unchanged. Check a new screen at 1280px as well as 390px.
- **The calendar is Month, Week and Day, with a Today button.** The switch is a thin `PillGroup` in `PeriodSwitcher`, the arrows step by the view's unit, Today appears only while today is outside the shown period, and the stats and tiles always describe the shown period, and the cost tile adds up the planned meals of that period, ahead of today as well as behind it, so it is "Cost" and never "Spent". A view that reaches past the loaded month window moves the listener's window; paging inside it never does.

- **Emoji carry meaning.** Meals are 🍳 breakfast, 🥪 lunch, 🍝 dinner, 🍿 snack, 🍰 dessert, 🥤 drink, in that order; the same six colors are the `--pinch-meal-*` tokens (and their `-soft` tints behind the calendar's chips and pills). Calories and macros are 🔥 calories (whole numbers), 💪 protein, 🌾 carbs and 🥑 fat in grams, everywhere they appear, through the shared `MacroStats` and its one emoji map `MACRO_EMOJIS` (inline in a row, tiles in a summary, `isCompact` tiles where space is tight), never a sentence of figures. A recipe's facts (🍽️ servings, 🔪 prep, ⏲️ cook) are one plain line of emoji and text, never pills; "cooked" is ✅, never a meal's emoji (🍳 is breakfast). Ingredients have no icon for now (the approach is being rethought), so a row is its name; never a sparkle for "other".

- **Missing details appear only as in-context nudge lines.** A missing price, package size or nutrition value is one quiet line where it changes a number ("2 ingredients need a price · Add prices"), with a close button and a short fill-in sheet. Never a to-do list, a badge, a required field or a setup step, and a total says what it leaves out through a warning icon beside it (`HelpTip variant='warning'`) that opens like the help icon ("This leaves out 2 ingredients because there's no price for them yet"), and the fix stays one tap away on the drawers.
- **Three layers, recipes name only the ingredient.** Family, ingredient, product. A recipe or meal refers to an ingredient id, never a store or product; prices live on products.
- **The nudge lives on the drawers, not in the plate builder.** The recipe, saved meal, day and planned meal views carry "Add prices" (closing the drawer before the fill-in sheet opens); the meal builder is a full-screen subview, so it shows "Cost $0.08" with the warning icon, which opens its small help modal on a phone (the one overlay allowed over a subview) and a tooltip on a computer, and nothing else opens over it. A frozen day has no nudge, since a new price doesn't change it by itself, and its warning, tile and rows say so ("This day has passed, so a price you add now counts from today on", "Fixed when the day passed", "Wasn't priced"); once a price would change it, the day's drawer offers "Price this day with today's prices" instead (the bullet on pricing a day again).
- **"Yours" is what you added, adjusted, priced or cook with.** Custom ingredients, adjusted catalog ones, ones with a product and ones a recipe, a saved meal or a planned day that isn't frozen yet uses; typing in the search looks through every ingredient, so a first-run kitchen never hears "nothing matches" for something the catalog has. Removing an in-use recipe, ingredient or store says it is taken off your lists, not erased, and a store the person adds back from the list comes back. The "Needs a price" and "Has a price" filters only look through Yours, and the filter panel says so, so a count of "22 ingredients" never reads as the whole catalog.
- **A new recipe asks how to start, with every way side by side, and each way is its own view.** A drawer on a phone, the page's own list from `sm` up (`MethodPicker`, rows only): From text, From a file ("A photo, screenshot or PDF"), From a link, then "Write it yourself" last and quieter. Picking one only picks: the text box, the file drop or the link field lives on its own screen (back raises the phone's drawer again, or returns to the list from `sm` up, and each says "Write it yourself instead"), never revealed under the list; the button reads "Fill in the recipe", and leaving a form that holds typed or read-in work asks Keep editing / Discard. The readings fill the real form for review and never save. Everything a recipe normally has (servings, times, photo, description, where it's from, ingredients, steps, how to keep it, nutrition) is a visible section of the form, never a "+ Add X" chip. Nutrition shows what was calculated per serving, lets the person change any number and reset to the calculated ones; a number equal to the calculated one stays derived.
- **The recipe form's editors are the drawers allowed over a subview.** The form's basics are summary rows, not boxes (so is "Keeping it", below the steps): Title, Prep and cook and Description each show a small caption, the value (the description clamped to two lines) and a chevron, and open their editor (`SummaryEditor`: a drawer on a phone or a short screen, a popover under the row from `sm`, each ending in Done; Enter in a one-line field closes it and never saves the recipe, and Escape or the back gesture closes only the editor). Servings stays an inline stepper. A recipe written from scratch opens its title editor once by itself; one from a reading, and any existing recipe, opens with every row closed. A bad value (minutes that aren't whole, a text over its limit) shows in red on its row as well as in the editor, and the pinned footer says what to fix. Prep and cook are two number fields, "Prep time" and "Cook time", each with a visible "min" suffix, never "(minutes)" in a label. On a phone adding and editing an ingredient (search, then amount, unit and note), a step (one text box) or the nutrition numbers (four small fields and "Reset to what we calculated") each happens in a drawer on top of the recipe form, ending in Done (and "Add another" while adding); a drawer holds one level, never opens another overlay, and from `sm` up a row opens in place. A screen's header carries the recipe's or meal's name as it is typed.
- **Taking a row out of a form is undoable for a few seconds.** Removing an ingredient or step from the recipe form (the ✕, the trash, or "Remove" in a drawer) or an item from the meal builder's plate takes it out at once and floats "Removed Salt · Undo · 6" above the pinned footer (`UndoSnackbar` over `useUndoRemoval`, counting 6 to 1): Undo puts the row back where it was, one removal is pending at a time (a newer one makes the older final), and the removal stands when the count ends. Whole-record deletes (a recipe, meal, ingredient, store, product) keep their confirm dialogs.
- **Nutrition is entered on the label's basis and stored per 100 g.** The ingredient form asks "Per [amount] [g | oz | serving]" above the four numbers, converts to `nutritionPer100g`, shows what it saved ("Saved as per 100 g: 🔥200") and refuses a blank or absurd number with one line. The serving option waits for a serving size that converts to grams. The ingredient drawer switches between per serving and per 100 g and hides the switch when both would show the same numbers.
- **Rows can hide their numbers, and start with the fewest.** Kitchen and Recipes/Meals have one View options control (an eye button, `ListViewControls` over the shared `ViewOptions`): show prices, show calories and macros, and the nutrition basis, remembered on the device per person. A list starts with prices on and calories and macros off (the eye button turns them on, and a choice already stored is kept); the macros switch is also off and disabled, with a line saying why, while no metric is on in Settings. Prices and macros are independent lines on a row, hiding one never changes a total, and the ingredient drawer's per serving / per 100 g switch is the same setting as the list's, so a row and its drawer never disagree. A row is as tall as its lines: a title-only ingredient row is about 44px, one with a price line about 60px, and a row with a leading tile or logo is as tall as the tile; never a `min-h` that leaves blank space under a lone title. `FamilyGroup` and the recipe list estimate their height from the lines that will show (price and nutrition), so the scrollbar doesn't jump when an option changes.
- **Every recipe reading discloses first.** The photo, text and link ways each open their own line ("An AI reads the picture and fills in the form. It isn't saved.") before anything is sent; a row the reader couldn't match keeps its amount, says "Not in your ingredients yet · tap to add or choose", and the footer says why Add is disabled.
- **A details drawer keeps its actions together.** The footer is Modify (secondary, left) beside the one primary (Add to calendar, Plan it) which takes the leftover width; the related rows in the body (a recipe's Start cooking, I cooked this, Add to shopping list and Cooking history; a planned meal's Start cooking, Mark as cooked and Add to shopping list) sit in one `ActionGroup` block with hairline dividers, never a stack of separately framed cards; the front matter is one plain emoji line (servings, prep, cook) and tiles (a Cost tile with its warning icon, emoji macro tiles), never pills or grey sentences; the ingredient drawer's line gives every fact an emoji (✏️ for yours, 🍽️ for the serving) and the family name its weight.
- **Where a recipe came from is a `Source`, never `Recipe.source`.** A source is an id and a label (`sources`, named by `Recipe.sourceId`); `source` only says how a draft was made. Readers go through `getRecipeSource`, so a missing key, `null` and a deleted source all mean "no source", and deleting a source clears `sourceId` on its recipes in batches without touching their `updatedAt` (it versions the cover) and never deletes a recipe. The recipe form's optional "Where it's from" is a visible `PickOrCreate` after the description that reuses a label already there (ignoring case) and, for a recipe read from a link, starts on the site without taking focus. The Sources entry (Kitchen's `EntryRow`) and the Recipes filter (a `FilterBar` with one Source question, pills with counts, "No source · n" only when some recipe has none) appear only once there is something to list, and a failed sources listener never reaches the load-error screen.
- **The shopping list is one line per ingredient and unit, filled from a sheet that picks what goes on it.** An item is `shoppingItems/{ingredientId}__{unit}`, so adding the same thing again merges: an unchecked line gains the amount (two decimals), a checked one starts again unchecked, a missing one is created, all in one `runTransaction` that reads every target first; units are never converted. Lines come from the pure `utils/shopping.ts` (a recipe scaled by servings over its own; a meal, planned meal or day: recipe items by their servings, ingredient items at their serving size) and are checked by `npm run check:pinch-math`. Ticking, changing and removing are field-scoped writes that touch only their own row (no sheet-wide busy), "Clear" is one `runTransaction` per chunk of 250 that re-reads each item and deletes only those still ticked (an item another device un-ticked survives) and reports how many it really cleared, and the 200 lines per add and the 300-item soft cap are the app's limits, not the rules'. A tick holds its row for 1.2 s (`SHOPPING_MOVE_DELAY_MS`): the write, the "to buy" subtitle, the tab badge and the cost follow at once, but the row stays where it was drawn, struck through, before it moves to "Got it" (an un-tick leaves "Got it" the same way), so the rows around it never shift under a second tap; each row has its own timer, the held rows sit in a ref that renders nothing when a row is held (only letting go renders), timers end with the screen, and "Clear" works on what is stored. A ticked line that changes unit stays ticked, and moving into a unit already on the list adds to an open line or restarts a ticked one, which the row sheet says before saving. The recipe, saved meal, planned meal and day drawers each carry a tinted "Add to shopping list" row in the body (not on a day that has passed) that closes the drawer and opens `AddToShoppingSheet` (titled "Shopping list", the verb is on the row and the button): a recipe gets a servings stepper, every line starts ticked except one already on the list ("On your list · 2 lb"), one scrolling list sits under a fixed header, and a toast says what was added with a "View" button. The list is the fourth tab (`ShoppingScreen`, no back button): grouped by family (the first three groups draw at once, the rest near the viewport, a group draws 100 rows then "Show N more", two columns from `lg`), with "Got it" collapsed beside its "Clear" and a search from 8 rows; its row and add sheets are ordinary `FormSheet`s over the tab. Its cost line says "Amounts on your list: about $3.16" with a help icon at all times (a warning when it leaves something out and names it and where to add the price): it prices the amounts from default products, not packages, so the till total is usually higher. The add toast's "View" switches to the tab only when no subview is open, otherwise it only dismisses, and both add sheets wait for the shopping listener (`isLoaded`, no `loadError`) before they add. A failed shopping listener logs, clears its items and degrades only its own screen and sheets.
- **A recipe idea is a link, a photo or a few words, kept until it becomes a recipe.** It is the third pill on Recipes (Recipes, Meals, Ideas; the header then reads Ideas with "New idea"). `ideas/{id}` holds `kind` (LINK, PHOTO, TEXT), title, note, url, siteName, imageUrl, photoPath and `recipeId`; the picture address is https only and a third party's, so it loads only through `FallbackImage` with the kind's tile (🔗 📷 📝) as the fallback. "New idea" is one `FormSheet` that swaps the `MethodPicker` list of ways for a way's own view with a "Back" link, never a second overlay. A link says what is fetched before anything is sent ("We fetch the page to find its title and picture. Nothing is saved from it except those."), is looked up through `fetchPageContent` for `meta` only (six seconds, a failure never cached as an answer), and saving waits for it to settle (Save is off with a footer note, six seconds at most), so a link is named after its page or the person is asked for a title: a failed, slow or title-less lookup shows the Title field, and a title left blank saves with the host. A link already on the list (`getComparableUrl`) opens that idea instead of saving a copy. The drawer's one primary is "Turn it into a recipe" (a link idea opens the recipe start's link way with its address already filled in, nothing sent until the person taps, and the reading fills the form for review keeping the idea's own title and note; "Write it yourself instead", a photo and a few words open the form with the title, the note as the description and the link's host as the source, "From your idea: …" above it; the recipe commits first on its own and the idea's `recipeId` is set by a separate write right after, whichever way the form was reached, whose failure is logged and never loses the recipe; a photo isn't copied and the form says so) or "Open the recipe" once made. A deleted or archived recipe reads as "not made yet", nothing cascades, the 200-idea cap is the app's and not the rules', and a failed ideas listener degrades only its own list ("New idea" stays on screen but disabled until the ideas have loaded, and `createIdea` refuses too). A save that fails says why as one red line in the pinned footer's `note`, and it clears when the form changes.
- **Cooking mode is one recipe, one step at a time, and writes only when told to.** "Start cooking" is a tinted row above "Add to shopping list" in the recipe drawer (only with a step) and in the planned meal view (only on a day that hasn't passed; with several recipes that have steps it swaps the drawer to a short chooser with a "‹ Back" link, never a second overlay). It closes the drawer and opens an overlay `Subview` (`kind: 'cook'`) whose header back, Escape and back gesture share one `leave()`: it closes at once, or asks "Stop timers and leave?" (Stay / Leave, naming how many) while a timer is running or paused, with a thin Steps / Ingredients `PillGroup` under the header: the step in `text-2xl`, Back and Next 56px tall and pinned (a compact row on a short landscape screen) with the timer strip above them, swipe and arrow keys, Finish on the last step. Finish swaps in a final panel, "Nice work. Mark it as cooked?", with the session's servings: "I cooked this" goes through the same leave question while a timer is live, writes the cook (the cooks bullet; started from a planned meal it marks that meal's own cook, or the panel says "Already marked as cooked" with Done when it exists), toasts with Undo and swaps the panel in place to "Marked as cooked" with a quiet "Add a note about it" (the notes bullet) and Done: the note's box opens in the panel with Cancel and Save note in the footer, its Cancel, the header back and Escape ask Keep editing / Discard while words are typed (not while it saves, nor once an Undo hides the box), the timers end once the cook is written, and every close after an awaited step is `closeSubviewIf`; "Not now", the header back, Escape and the back gesture write nothing, and a quiet "Back to the last step" link returns to the step. Finish only opens it while the cooks listener has landed without error, else it leaves as before. When the recipe has notes, a quiet block under the switch (not on the final panel) says "Your latest note (Oct 12): …" in two lines, or "Changed on Oct 6: …" when the newest entry is a tweak, and a tap opens the notes in place, never a sheet over the screen. Amounts scale through `scaleAmount`, the shopping list's own math, units never converted, and the ticks, step and servings live in the session. Times in a step (`findDurations`, a general util) become dashed `SuggestionChips` that start up to four live timers (running or paused; a finished one waits for Dismiss and doesn't count) running to a clock deadline, ending with a toast, a buzz and a beep that all fail quietly; leaving ends them and the screen says "Keep this screen open: timers stop if you leave it." once. A recipe changed or removed elsewhere keeps the session going on what it last had, with one quiet line saying so. `useWakeLock` keeps the screen on and, only when it can't, one line says "This phone may turn the screen off; timers keep running only while this screen is open." in place of the timer line. Its only writes are that cook and a note about it: no other Firestore, indexes, Storage or `localStorage`.

- **A cook is a quiet record that a recipe was made.** `cooks/{id}` holds `recipeId`, `plannedMealId`, `servings`, `cookedAt` (an instant, shown in local time) and the two times, owner only, one document each. The ways in are the recipe drawer's tinted "I cooked this" row (one tap at the recipe's own servings and a toast "Marked Adobo chicken as cooked" with an Undo that deletes exactly that cook; the drawer then says "✅ Cooked 3 times · last Oct 12" under its facts and gains a "Cooking history" row) and, on today or a day that has passed, a "Mark as cooked" row per recipe in a planned meal (now for today, local noon of that day for a past one; the id `{plannedMealId}__{recipeId}` and a `runTransaction` make a second mark "Already marked", and the row then reads "Cooked ✓ · Undo", whose Undo asks first like the history's trash because it can come weeks later, and cooking mode started from that meal finishes into the same cook). The Recipes row adds a muted "Cooked 3×" only above zero, and each row subscribes to its own count (a number) so one mark re-renders one row. The history is a `FormSheet` whose rows open one cook's day, time and servings in place (a "Back" link, a field-scoped `updateDoc`, an icon-only trash with a destructive confirm that names the day) and "+ Add an earlier cook"; it draws 8 rows and "Show N more" only when more than three would hide. The 500-cooks-per-recipe cap is the app's, not the rules'; a failed cooks listener degrades only its own UI (no row, no line); archiving a recipe keeps its cooks and a hard delete finds them with a query on the recipe (the store can be empty) and removes them in batches, best effort, since every reader ignores a cook whose recipe is gone. Toasts sit above drawers (`index.css`), so an Undo raised from one can be tapped. A planned meal's mark is a transaction, which needs a connection, so a failed cook write says "Check your connection and try again."; the drawer's "I cooked this" row is disabled until its write settles, and a write that resolves late never closes a screen it didn't open (`closeSubviewIf`).
- **A recipe's notes are a journal, and a tweak is the dated record of an edit.** `notes/{id}` holds `recipeId`, `kind` (NOTE or TWEAK), `text`, `changes`, `cookId` and the two times, owner only; a NOTE is the person's words (always `text`, optionally tied to the cook it follows, and a deleted cook leaves a plain note), a TWEAK is written by `saveRecipe` in the recipe's own `writeBatch` when the recipe has a cook and the edit changed what the form edits (never a new recipe, a never-cooked one, a cover-only edit or while the cooks or notes listener hasn't landed), its `changes` computed at save time by the pure `getRecipeChanges` (at most 8 plain lines, "and N more" last) and immutable, its `text` the optional "What changed and why?" line. `Recipe` has no field for it: "Last tweaked Oct 20" is the newest TWEAK, and a NOTE older than it says "Written before your Oct 20 tweak". The drawer's "Notes" section sits under the steps (one "Add a note" row while empty; then the row, the entries newest first, a TWEAK's heading "Changed on Oct 20" beside a small "Tweak" tag, five shown and "Show N more" only when more than three would hide, text clamped at four lines with "More"); "Add a note" and Edit close the drawer and open the `FormSheet` "Note" (the words, and "Is this about a time you cooked it?" as pills only for a cooked recipe, the trash with a destructive confirm, "Update the recipe from this note" which opens the recipe form with the note quoted and a new TWEAK on save, the NOTE untouched). Each cook in the history has an "Add a note" that swaps in place, and cooking mode's last screen offers "Add a note about it" typed in place, never a sheet over the subview. An edit saves only `text` and `updatedAt`; an add writes a fresh id with `setDoc`; both wait for the notes listener (`isLoaded`, no `loadError`) and a close that follows an awaited write (the sheet, the history's note view, the cooking screen) is a functional update on its own target, so a late save never closes what the person has opened since; a hard delete reads the recipe's notes from Firestore (the store is empty while they load) and removes them in batches, best effort, and an archive keeps them; the 300 per recipe is the app's cap, not the rules'; a failed notes listener logs, clears its items and takes away only the Notes section's add row (the recipe still saves, without a tweak).
- **A recipe's care is one optional row, one drawer section and one line.** `Recipe.care` is `{ fridgeDays, freezerDays, reheat, tip } | null` (days whole, 1 to 365; reheat and tip at most 300 characters each), written by every save of the recipe, so a recipe from before it (no key) gains it as null, and read through `getRecipeCare`, which treats a missing key, null and a map of nothing alike. The form's "Keeping it" row sits between Steps and Nutrition (`RecipeCareField`, a `SummaryEditor`: two "days" fields, a reheating line and a tip, small captions and 40px fields; its summary reads "Fridge 3 days, freezer 3 months, reheating, tip", joined with commas so a wrap never leaves a dot at a line's end; days are typed as days and shown as weeks or months when they fit, a month being 30). The drawer's "Keeping it" section (🧊 ❄️ ♨️ 💡 lines in a 20px emoji column, the reheat one never 🔥, which is calories) sits between Steps and Notes and exists only when there is something to say, and cooking mode's last screen gains the one muted line "Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge, 3 months in the freezer" (days only) in both its states. The three readings fill it for review from what the source states and never an estimate, a range as its shorter end (a safety figure, unlike a cooking timer's longer end), weeks and months as days rounded down, anything outside 1 to 365 dropped; a reading with none leaves it null and adds nothing to the "couldn't read" list. A change to it never records a tweak (storage advice isn't a change to the dish, and a tweak would move "Last tweaked" and relabel older notes), and Pinch has no copy-out, so nothing carries it away.

- **Prepared meals are containers: one document per physical container, owner only, kept until finished.** `containers/{id}` (a `Container`) holds `title`, `recipeId` / `mealId` / `cookId` (immutable, never a recipe and a meal together), `servings` (whole portions, 1 to 100), `status` (`IN_STOCK` or `DONE`), `place` (`FRIDGE` or `FREEZER`), `eatBy` and a `history` of at most 10 events (MADE, FRIDGE, FREEZE, THAW, EAT, TOSS, EDIT; the rules can only walk a list by index and the evaluator stops at 1,000 expressions, so a longer one drops the oldest EAT first, then the oldest EDIT, then, in a long run of moves, the oldest thaw after the first place event; what went in and every freeze stay). A container is never split (a put-away of portions in separate boxes writes one to twenty containers in one batch, each its own history): eating some lowers `servings`, the last portion or a toss sets `DONE` and keeps the document as history, and Delete is its own destructive action that says the history goes with it. `eatBy` is a date-only value (UTC midnight, `fromDateInputValue`, shown with the planned-day helpers); "Eat soon" is today and the next two days in the viewer's local time and "Past its date" is before today; event times are instants. Every change that depends on the container's own state is a `runTransaction` over a pure plan in `utils/containers.ts` and says in one plain sentence when it can't happen. The default eat-by comes from the recipe's `care` or a named default, is shown as the person's own call ("Your call"), and no copy says food is safe. A move starts from the date the container already has: past its date stays past through any move and the view says so, a thaw never starts later than it, and a refreeze says it has been frozen before. Only `IN_STOCK` is listened to (`startContainersListener`, from `usePinchSync`, an optional extra: its failure logs, clears the list and shows one line on its own surfaces, never the load-error screen); Finished is read on demand through TanStack Query (the `status` + `updatedAt` index); scoped to one recipe or meal it reads that recipe's or meal's own containers (one equality, at most 200), so an older finished one is never hidden behind the newest 25. A saved meal's stock includes its own recipes' (`getMealStockMap`), and a recipe whose only use is in-stock containers is archived, not erased. Stock shows as `StockLine` on a recipe or meal row and `StockRows` in its drawer ("In your freezer · 3 servings, eat by Nov 3", "Put some away"), and Recipes and Meals have an "In stock" filter that says so on its active chip. Cooking mode asks "Putting any away?" only for a bulk meal (`isBulkMeal`: five or more servings, a recipe that says it freezes, or one already in stock) that has something left after tonight and what this cook already put away, and otherwise offers a quiet "Put some away" link; nothing is written until Save (which never holds the screen waiting for the server), a note saved first keeps the question, and the containers carry the `cookId`. Every container action and every Undo ignores a second tap (an Undo runs once per toast), and a container sheet opens with focus on Done, never on an action. Prepared meals live under Kitchen: no Calendar or Overview surface and no push for an eat-by date.


- **A passed day is priced again only when the person asks, and nothing else about it changes.** Its meals keep the `snapshot` the app froze them with; "Price this day with today's prices" is the one thing that replaces it. It is a tinted `ActionRow` with a help icon (`trailing`) in the day drawer and the planned meal view, shown only for a day that has passed, with the kitchen loaded, when today's prices would change a total, a line or what it leaves out (`wouldRepriceChange`, never for today, a day ahead, an unchanged or empty day). Its subtitle, help text and confirm say it redoes the day's cost and nutrition from today's prices and recipes (a changed recipe can move the day, calories included), true even for a day that had no prices. It asks first with a destructive confirm that previews the result ("Before" in the cost tile's words and "After" as "about $0.66", one figure per line, and how many ingredients still have no price only above zero, worked out at the tap by `getRepricePreview` with the function the write uses) and says it can't be undone ("Use today's prices for Oct 12?", no Undo), then one `runTransaction` per day (`repriceDay`) re-reads each meal and replaces only `snapshot` and `updatedAt`, so planned meal ids, and the cooks that hang off them, stay; the row is disabled until it settles, a failure is one plain sentence under it, and a toast says "Oct 12 now uses today's prices". `snapshot.repricedAt` (null when the app froze the day, an instant when the person asked; older snapshots lack the key and read as null) is what the rules require to replace a snapshot and what the copy reads: "Fixed when the day passed" and "Wasn't priced" until then, "Priced with today's prices on Oct 14" and "Still no price" after, never "as they were that day". The app's own freezing never touches a snapshot that exists.
- **State the person expects back after switching views lives in the session, not in a conditionally rendered view.** Cooking mode's ticks, step and servings sit in `CookingSession` beside the timers, so going to Ingredients and back (or to Steps and back) keeps them, and they end with the screen; a view that unmounts keeps nothing of its own that the person would miss.

- **A stored picture's cache version is a field the picture owns.** A download URL is cached by path and a version; when the version is an `updatedAt` that any edit touches, `useStorageImageUrl` keeps the previous picture on screen for the same path while the new one loads, so editing a note never flashes the thumbnail to its placeholder.

- **Vendored assets keep their licence.** Pinch vendors nothing right now (the ingredient icons were removed); a store logo comes from Logo.dev and is credited in Settings' About row. Add any new vendored set with its notice beside it.
- **Check lists at scale.** Pinch needs an oversized seed fixture like Waypoint's (`scripts/seeds/pinchScale.ts`, "Big Kitchen (scale test)"). Open new lists on it at phone width with the dev build throttled, and keep per-row work cheap (CLAUDE.md "Performance is a design check"): catalog groups mount only near the viewport (`LazyMount` plus `defer-offscreen`), and a long list (Products, a week's agenda) caps its rows with "Show N more".
