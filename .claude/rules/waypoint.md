# Waypoint rules

Applies on top of CLAUDE.md to everything under `src/apps/waypoint/`. Keep `.github/instructions/waypoint.instructions.md` (the source of truth) identical to this body.

- **Theme: business casual.** Utility with a little style, never cute and never corporate. The palette (a quiet steel-blue primary, sky-tinted neutrals, a warm orange accent) lives in `waypoint.css` as tokens on `html.waypoint-theme`, switched on by `useWaypointTheme()` while the app is mounted so portaled modals and drawers match. Change the look there, not per component, and use semantic tokens (`bg-primary`, `bg-secondary`, `bg-accent`) rather than raw colors. The primary stays quiet on purpose: it is on every card button, so a loud one overwhelms a list of trips. The one deliberate exception is the live "now" signal (the pill's emerald dot and outline), which means "happening right now". Keep cute emoji off utility surfaces (invitations, confirmations); emoji carry meaning on event types, roles and sections.
- **Corners sit between formal and playful, set by the tokens.** The theme sets `--radius-md` and up, and the same file insets the text of inputs and select triggers (`padding-inline`) so nothing presses against a curve; don't hand-roll radii or padding. Pills (`@/components/Pill`) are for options a user picks among (event type, transit type, meal, who's coming), never a `Select` of three to six items, radio buttons or a tab strip; a selected pill is a tint with a primary outline (`[data-pill][aria-pressed]` in `waypoint.css`), so its emoji and label stay readable. A `Select` stays for long lists (days, time zones, airlines).
- **Emoji carry meaning.** Event types, transit types, meals and sections each have their own emoji (`EVENT_TYPE_EMOJIS`, `TRANSIT_TYPE_EMOJIS`); never reuse one across a list.
- **Forms ask little up front, and long ones are not modals.** Phone first: a short form (create trip, title, dates, announcement, join code, add a member by email) is a `Modal`; anything that can scroll on a phone (stay, rental, expense, checklist item, idea, suggestion, split) is a `FormSheet`: a tall drawer on phones, so the screen behind stays in view, and a modal from `sm` up. The event form is the one page-sized form and stays a `FormScreen` (a full-screen overlay subview); revisit it if it feels like a takeover. Read-only detail of unpredictable length (notes, dues) is a `DetailSheet`. An item's details drawer closes before its editor opens. The form starts with the essentials, then the details the journey depends on are asked as visible `Pill` questions, and only truly optional extras are "+ Add X" chips (`AddFieldChips`/`RemovableField`). Asked, not tucked away: who's coming (it filters everyone's Overview), "Know when you arrive?" for a travel leg, "Already booked?" for a stay or rental (confirmation and link), "Returning it somewhere else?" for a rental. Titles are plain nouns, section buttons are just "Add", and footers are `ModalFooterActions` with Cancel on the left (`cancelAction`), a `DeleteIconButton` beside it and the primary action alone on the right.
- **Time is trip-relative, and a day can sit just outside the trip.** A day number may run from 3 days before the first day to 3 days after the last (`MAX_DAYS_OUTSIDE_TRIP`), for the travel days around a trip; a checklist item may be due on any day, however far before, and stays relative to the trip's start when dates move. A start and an end may be in different time zones (`endTimezone`; a flight's landing zone); the zone picker searches by city or zone name (`@/utils/timezoneSearch`) and never assumes a fixed UTC offset, so daylight saving (and places without it, like Phoenix) come from the runtime's zone database.
- **Overview is about me.** Today, the Now / Up next pill and the agenda list only events the viewer is part of (`isEventForMember`); the Timeline shows everything. Before the trip, Overview asks each member for their own arrival and departure (`TravelPrompts`), skippable and quiet afterwards.
- **People can be added by email before they join.** An Admin taps Add in the Members header, enters an email and a role (`apps/waypoint/emailInvites`); nothing is sent. That person never requests access: after they sign in, an invitation modal opens on My Trips (Join, Not for me, Close), and after Close it waits behind an icon button beside Join with code. Added emails show in the Members list as "yet to join", with a remove button for Admins. Joining adds them and deletes the entry in one batch.
- **Expenses can be paid early.** A member in an expected expense's split records money already sent someone ("I paid early", `EarlyPaymentModal`): the recipient is picked (never typed), the amount can't pass the sender's share (an estimate caps at the share of its maximum and defaults to the share of its minimum), and it nets out of the Dues summary until it is returned. Totals have three pill views: Per person, Group, Mine. The Dues summary opens with notices about the signed-in person's early payments, and its pair sheet shows how the net adds up; the author or an editor can remove an early payment.
- **A price says who pays it, in one question.** "How much is it?" holds the whole price: Exact amount or Estimate (a range), the amount in one full-width box whose label reads "Total amount" or "Amount per person" with a quiet "Switch to per person" / "Switch to total" link beside it (no pills for a two-way switch), and a line under it that works the example out ("$120.00 shared by 5 people is $24.00 each"); a line stays true for a split among only some people, and a private or single-person expense isn't asked who the price is for. "Trip day" is a `Select` like the event form, and Group and Note are the same "Add to this expense" chips as the event form. A trip-dates change that moves the start asks "What happens to your plans?" with pills and says the effect in a line ("Every plan slides 3 days later with the trip and keeps its day number", and for the other pill a real example from this trip); the checklist note is a top-placed help tooltip beside the question's label, never radio buttons.
- **An expense asks what it pays for first, then reveals the rest in stages.** The add form opens with one question, "What's this expense for?" (the events, stays and rentals that have no expense yet as pills: two rows on a phone with "Show all N", and a search past 12, like the other long pill lists, under a row of thin kind filters (All, ✈️ Travel, 🎒 Activities, 🍽️ Dining, 🏨 Stays, 🚘 Rentals, and Other as the catch-all bucket, which for now holds free time; a filter with nothing in it is hidden), because a trip has many events and few stays or rentals; then a filled "Something else" button below them); nothing else shows until it is answered, and a trip with no plans, a Commenter's private form and the Just for me Add skip it. A picked plan shows as a "Paying for" summary (its own title, category and day, no emoji of ours; "Change", which returns to the question with the plan selected, and "Edit title or category") instead of fields, then come "Who's this for?" (Everyone / Pick people / Just me, which keeps what was typed and turns the form into a private personal expense in place), "How much is it?" (Exact amount or Estimate, the amount and Total / Per person) and "Add to this expense" chips for the rest: Trip day (hidden when the plan has one), Group, Note. An expense's details and its payment are two separate matters with their own sheets: the details form never asks or shows whether it is paid (adding or editing), and payment is its own step. Payment is chosen where the expense is added, with no step after it: the footer's primary is "Add", and above it a full-width "Add and mark paid" (exact amounts only; recorded as paid by the person adding it; a personal expense is just marked paid) saves it already paid. Anyone else paying, or paying later, uses "Mark paid" in the expense's details, whose "Paid by" is a row of pills (Each person, then the members) and which also asks for the amount when it was an estimate. "Mark as unpaid" (confirm) lives beside "Mark paid" in the expense's details, and a personal expense's editor has the pair at the top. Both are transactions that re-read the expense, so a stale screen never overwrites someone else's change, and unpaid clears everyone's "repaid" marks. Someone who answered "Something else" can still tap "Choose from your itinerary" without losing what they typed, and flipping the audience never sends them back to the first question. Picking another plan replaces what the first one filled but never what the person typed, and a day a plan filled stays visible once the link is gone. Editing an expense shows its fields with the chips already open and can change or clear the link, and only fills a missing day. An activity (`EXPENSE_TRACKED_EVENT_TYPES`), stay or rental with no expense attached shows a quiet "💸 Not paid for yet" tab on the card's bottom-right corner and a badge in its details, and the Timeline's View options have "Only activities not paid for yet". A link whose target was deleted reads as none. For an Admin or Editor the badge is also the way in (`LinkExpenseSheet`): it lists the expenses not yet linked to anything (those on the same day first, searchable from 8), and tapping one attaches it without changing anything it has, filling in only a day it lacks; "Add a new expense" opens the form already filled in. Inside a details drawer the badge is plain text, since no sheet opens over a drawer.
- **Personal expenses are private and added on top, never mixed in.** "Just me" in the add form (or the Add of "Just for me", a section above the Dues summary so it is found without scrolling past dues, with a search from 8 rows and rows that open the editor) holds expenses only the signed-in person can see: they live under their own uid (`apps/waypoint/personalExpenses/{uid}/items`), so no one else's list, totals or dues ever include them, and any trip member (a Commenter too, whose form is only ever private) can keep them; deleting the trip removes them for its current members. The Expenses totals keep showing what everyone sees; when a card's personal amount is above zero it adds a soft accent `+ $X personal` chip and `= $Y` beneath, in every view (Per person, Group, Mine), and one muted line under the view hint says the orange amount is theirs alone. The Mine view's hint is one short line with a "What do these mean?" link that opens the explanation; the trip list's header says "trip expenses" once personal ones exist.
- **The itinerary copies out as Markdown.** "Copy trip as Markdown" sits in the trip menu (⋯) for every member, and a "Copy as Markdown" button sits beside View options on the Timeline from `sm` up, and on phones a copy icon sits in the trip header on the Timeline tab, apart from the title; both build the text at the tap (`utils/itineraryMarkdown.ts`), toast when it is on the clipboard, and ignore the view options and filters, so the copy is always the whole plan. The Timeline copy is one heading per day with stays, rentals and plans placed by time (archived events left out); the trip copy adds the dates, city, time zone and people, then the Timeline, Stays, Car rentals, Checklist and Ideas. Expenses and dues are never copied (money, and some of it private), member emails never are (a member with no name reads "Trip member"), and a long list of names is cut after ten ("and 20 more").
- **The Expenses view reads in three matching blocks.** "Just for me", "Dues summary" and "All expenses" share one header (icon, title, a muted subtitle, a small action at the right), so the page has one hierarchy; the dues subtitle carries the count ("2 to settle") and the body never repeats it. Just for me shows three rows and "View all N" (a drawer on phones, inline from `sm` up; a row opened from the drawer closes it first), rows are `Clickable` so a tap never flashes a button background, and every expense row leads with a round tinted category emoji. "Paid early" is one quiet row on phones that opens a drawer (the same icon and sentence as the desktop block), whose only action is "Undo" for the person who sent it.
- **Only a scheduled trip asks when you arrive.** A flight (🛬 "Know when you land?"), a train (🚉) or a ferry (⚓) asks "Know when you arrive?" with its own wording (`TRANSIT_ARRIVAL`); a drive, walk, ride or other leg just offers an optional end time. The travel prompt on Overview can be dismissed for good ("Don't ask again").
- **A checklist item is asked like an expense.** The form opens on the task and its category pills, then "Who's on it?" (Everyone / Pick people, the people as pills), and only "Complete by" and "Note" are "Add to this task" chips with a remove. Rows show the task, a muted emoji-and-category line, and avatars or one round people icon for everyone.
- **A linked expense offers to follow its item, quietly.** When the expense's title or day differs from the itinerary item it is linked to, small muted right-aligned text under the field says "Use the itinerary item title" / "Use the itinerary item day" and sets it on tap; nothing is overwritten on its own.
- **A day's header rides along on a phone.** On the Timeline the day divider sticks under the app bar (`sticky-day-bar`, following the site header as it hides and returns) until the next day's header takes over, and while stuck it turns into a compact centered bar (`DayHeader`): the same height as the divider, so nothing jumps, with a blurred background and one hairline. From `sm` up it stays a plain divider.
- **An event card's top reads the same every time.** Line one is always the type badge and the time (truncating, never wrapping), with the weather chip and the notes dot pinned to its right; a second line exists only when there is something to put on it (archived); "Not paid for yet" is a tab hanging off the card's bottom-right corner, outside it, on a phone (the same on stay and rental cards) and a badge in the card's bottom-right corner from `sm` up; plain text inside a details drawer; an activity's indoor/outdoor setting is left to the details drawer on a phone; then the title. Optional fields never push the badge and time onto different lines.
- **The Timeline can be searched, trimmed and reset.** A search above the day picker filters plans by title, place, address and note (and stays and rentals by name) and hides weather-only days while it is typed; "Hide travel, stays and rentals" in View options leaves only the plans; and View options ends with "Reset to defaults" while anything differs. The Ideas card says "All your activities are on the itinerary" (not "No activities yet") when every idea of that kind has been scheduled.
- **Places already on the itinerary are one tap away.** Every leg but a flight (drive, train, ferry, walk, bike, scooter, other) shows "Or pick a place on your itinerary" under its location: the stays, rentals (pickup and return) and events that have an address, one pill per address (two rows then "Show all N", a search past 12, at every width), which fills the name, address and coordinates (`ItineraryPlacePicks`).
- **An idea becomes a place, not a title.** Adding an idea to the itinerary first looks the idea up in Places (`ideaPlaceQueryOptions`): a hit fills the location and address and leaves the title blank so the place's name is the title; only when nothing is found does the idea's title become the event's custom title.
- **Saving something offers the rest, quietly.** After a new event, stay or rental is saved (an idea converted to an event too), a sheet (`AddRelatedFlow`, hosted by `RelatedFlowProvider` so it outlives the card that saved it) offers "Add an expense" and "Add a checklist item", each opening its form already filled in and linked (title, day, a matching category) and returning to the sheet with a "✓ added" mark; Done is always there, nothing is added unless the person adds it, and only Admins and Editors see it. Free time, a walk, bike or scooter leg, and a leg saved with "Add another flight" don't open it.
- **A day's weather opens its details.** Tapping a weather card (the strip, the day's card or its compact line, Overview's today card) opens a sheet with the full weather backdrop, high and low, chance of rain, the place the forecast is for ("Based on" the main place of the day's located plans, else the trip's city) and, when the day spans places, an "Also on this day" list and every hour of the day, opening at 7 AM; its one button, "Sounds good", only closes it, so it never moves the screen behind. A trip can have a city (a searchable select like the time zone's, in the New trip form and under "Set city" in the trip menu, whose explanation is two plain sentences with the key phrases in bold; shown under the title on the trip card and in the header); the time zone is set at creation, and the city fills only days with no located plan (without a city, such a day shows no weather at the top; events keep their own chip). A day's weather follows its own plans: plans within 35 km are one place, a day in another place shows that place's weather, and a day split between places shows the main place (most plans; the later one when tied, since that is where the day ends) plus "Also <place>" chips, the place named under each strip tile and a "+N more" marker and a "+1" on the compact line; an event outside its day's main place names its place on its chip. Travel legs never say where a day is, and events stay in time order whatever their place.
- **Stays, rentals and travel ride the timeline, slim.** Each day shows a one-line row for a stay's check-in and check-out, a rental's pick-up (🚘, the car facing you) and return, and every travel leg (its transit emoji and title, with its time), placed by time among the day's events: the arrival or departure the traveler set (labelled Arrive/Leave) wins over the official check-in/out, and a stay with a day but no time sits at the top of the day, under its weather. Tapping a row opens its details (a drawer on phones, a modal from `sm` up): a stay's or rental's is read-only, a travel leg's keeps its Modify, stack and archive actions, and "Suggest a change" lives in those opened details (on phones, and for travel legs everywhere) rather than as a line under every collapsed card; pending suggestions still show under their card. The "Only activities not paid for yet" filter hides stays and rentals.
- **Split presets fit the group.** Beside a share's 25/50/75/100% shortcuts, the even share of the people in the split is added when it isn't one of them (20% of five, 33.3% of three, one decimal), and it applies the exact fraction.
- **Tappable rows read as text, not links.** Inside Waypoint a tertiary button's text is the normal text color (`waypoint.css`), so a screen of tappable rows is not a screen of blue; only real links (a URL, a place) are blue.
- **A tappable row lines up with the header above it.** On a phone the row's title starts at the section header's left edge and its chevron ends at the right edge (`px-0!`: a plain `px-0` loses to the theme's padding for full-width `justify-between` buttons). Measure it (`row title x == header x`, `chevron right == container right`) before calling a list done.
- **Lists open on tap on a phone.** An expense row shows title, category and status, and the amount; tapping it opens a details drawer with the split, early payments and the actions (Mark paid, Edit split, Modify). From `sm` up the row shows everything and its actions inline. The Dues summary leads with money to send and money to collect for the signed-in person, and tucks everyone else's pairs below.
- **Dues are quiet and collapsible.** The Dues summary sits in a `Disclosure` (open by default) so it can be folded away. Early payments are one "Paid early" block with the help line written once, then a compact row per payment (who sent whom, how much, which expense), newest first and only while they still offset something; a payment leaves the block once the expense is paid or it is sent back. Grouped expenses show their header and total smaller and muted so the rows inside lead. A range that does not fit wraps at its dash instead of overflowing.
- **Roles are picked with pills.** A member's role in the phone drawer is a `PillGroup` with the role's description beneath it, never radio buttons. Members and the checklist add a `SearchInput` from 8 rows (`LIST_SEARCH_THRESHOLD`).
- **A totals view shows its basis on the rows beneath it.** In Mine, each phone row carries the viewer's own share under the full price ("Mine $18.00"), so the rows add up to the header; a view that changes basis never leaves rows at a different one with no label.
- **A stack's range reads in the trip's time zone.** The span on a stack's header (earliest start through latest end) is shown on the trip's own calendar and zone with its abbreviation (`toTripDayTime`), so a glance says which day it was; each card inside keeps its event's own zone. On a phone the trip's header is a `StickyAppBar`: the title joins the back button once scrolled.
- **The scale fixture.** "Big Group Reunion (scale test)" (`seed-waypoint-trip-scale`, code `BIGGROUP`, `scripts/seeds/waypointScale.ts`) is the trip for checking that screens scale and stay fast: 120 members, a live 14-day timeline with stacks and groups, 60 stays, 30 rentals, 250 checklist items, 60 ideas and 150 expenses with ranges and early payments. Whenever a feature adds a list, section or per-member field, extend this seed with an oversized version in the same PR (and its document count), then open the feature on this trip at phone width, check it still reads well, and check that switching tabs stays snappy (Timeline and Expenses mount a day only near the viewport via `LazyMount`; a new long list does the same).
- **Days name their weekday.** Every day label (`getDayLabel`, `getDayDateLabel`) reads "Day 2 · Mon, Oct 5", in the form selects, tabs, filters and dividers alike; the weather strip draws its own weekday and passes `withWeekday: false`.
- **A document fills a form.** A flight, a stay or a rental form offers a compact "Have your confirmation?" row with an Upload button when creating (stays and rentals at the top; a flight right under its type pills): a photo, screenshot or PDF (`@apps/waypoint/lib/extractBookingFromFile`, through `UploadAutofill`) fills the real fields in, what couldn't be read is named, and the person edits and saves as usual. Flights only for travel. There is no general upload button and no checklist of proposed entries. A flight with no title of its own is named from its route ("Flight DL 482 · SEA → JFK").
- **What is happening now floats.** During a live trip a pill (`NowPill`) floats over every main screen showing what is happening and, with one button, what is next; a tap opens the details (a drawer on phones, a modal on a computer). Overview keeps the static hub: today's check-ins and pickups, weather, the day's agenda, and the entries to stays, rentals and ideas.
- **The time zone picker is quiet.** It searches a city or a zone name; a well-known city that isn't the zone's own (Milwaukee for Central Time) appears as its own option while typed; phones get short names ("PT") and the placeholder stays short. Raw zone ids with underscores are never shown.
- **Stacks and groups.** A group has its own stack button; stacking a grouped event says it stacks the whole group. A stack of one event gets the same header, border and hierarchy as a stack of many.
- **No "Back home" in the page.** The app shell gives Home through the avatar menu and the header's home icon. Only the landing/fallback page says "Back home".
