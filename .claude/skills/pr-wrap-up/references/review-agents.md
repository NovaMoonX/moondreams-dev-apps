# Review agents (technical, product, design)

Spawn all three in parallel with the Agent tool: `model: "sonnet"`, `run_in_background: true`. They are **read-only**: they report findings and the orchestrator fixes. Before spawning the product and design agents, the orchestrator has the emulators, the seeded data (Alex full, Jamie empty, Taylor scale) and a dev server running on a **frozen worktree at the head commit** (fixes made meanwhile must not move what they read; a "before" build on a second port for comparisons). Agents never reset or reseed data and never edit repo files.

## Keeping these sections useful

Revise these sections **on your own, without being asked**, whenever you are confident a change makes a review catch more, and in the same turn as the work that showed it. Any moment of feedback is a signal to re-evaluate them: the owner reporting a problem on a screen a review passed (whether or not they say "the agent should have caught this"), the owner correcting a design or product choice, a finding the owner rejects (a check that is wrong or noisy), a bug you or an implementer find after a review passed, and an audit or test that turns up a class of problem. For each, ask which agent's section should have caught it or should stop flagging it, and change **that agent's section** as an audit of the whole section, never an append:
1. Name the **class** of failure (not the instance) and why the section missed it: no check, a check too vague to act on, a check buried among many, or one that measured the wrong thing.
2. Put it in the group that owns the class and **sharpen that check** (add the measurement that would have caught it); add a group only when none fits.
3. While in the section: merge checks that overlap, replace checks the new one subsumes, delete checks that only restate a `CLAUDE.md` rule (point to the rule instead) or never caught anything, and fix stale wording. `polish-agents.md` and `SKILL.md` point here; they never keep their own copy of a check.
4. Keep each role to about ten groups an agent can run in full, each a test with its measurement and the owner round that taught it. A section that only grows stops being run.
5. A finding the owner rejected, or a check that keeps flagging what the owner accepts, is deleted or narrowed the same way: a check that misleads costs as much as one that is missing.
6. Leave a change out only when you are not confident it is right (one ambiguous instance, a preference the owner has not stated); then put it to the owner as a one-line proposal instead.
7. Tell the owner in one line what was added, merged, replaced and deleted, and why.

Screenshot **every subview and sheet reachable from the changed shared parts, not only the diff's**, beside three of those, at 390 light and dark, 320 and 844×390, and print for each its rest-state **empty share** (px between the last content and the pinned footer as a % of the viewport; over 30% needs a friendly empty card saying what comes next; a screen that is a list of the person's own records follows their data, so judge it at 5 and 50 rows and by kinds and the link test), its **treatment kinds** (framed card, tinted block, icon chip, pill, chevron row, emoji: at least three) and the **link test** (every underlined button that is not inside a sentence of three or more other words is a finding, one beside a chip included: a link is only inside a sentence; "Show N more" is `ShowMoreButton` and a reveal or two-way switch is `RevealChip`; a control that changes what the screen asks, opens another view or acts in bulk is a row with a chevron, a chip or a pill). 11. **Result cards and charts are judged as a whole, for noise and for fit with the app, not only for bugs (owner, round 15: the A-List projection shipped a library-default chart and a card of five muted lines past a pass that only hunted defects).** Screenshot each result card and chart at 390 light and dark and answer in writing before measuring:
    - **Text budget:** list every line and figure in the card in order and class each decision / status / reference / noise for the moment. A card carries one headline figure, one verdict chip and at most one line of context; every further line is conditional on a state that needs it (a caveat shows only when it applies). A line that restates the headline or the controls, a figure shown twice, or a baseline the chart already draws is noise; print the visible words beside the headline (over about forty is a finding).
    - **Chart look against the app's shape language:** a library-default chart (grid lines, axis lines, tick marks, rotated axis titles, square tooltip, a dot per point, more than three or four ticks an axis) in a rounded, playful app is a finding even when nothing is broken. Print the grid line count, ticks per axis, axis-line and tick-mark presence, stroke caps, fill and tooltip radius, and compare with the quiet look (`TrendLineChart isSoft`: no grid or axis lines, at most three ticks, round caps, a soft fill, one end dot, the end value in the headline).
    - **Everything is inside the plot in every state:** print the plot box and each series' extent with all values negative, with a guide line outside the data and with a comparison series far from the main one; every series and guide is drawn (not clipped or discarded) and no label sits on a line (label rect against the path).

    - **Reading a value off the chart:** a tap and a horizontal drag on touch (CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, `hasTouch`) and a mouse hover each show the day and every series' amount at that x, with a visible cursor line, and the readout follows the finger across the plot; vertical swipes still scroll the page (`touch-action: pan-y` on the chart box: print it and the page's `scrollY` before and after a horizontal drag).     - **The control that drives a chart visibly moves it (owner, round 18: stepping a pace up barely changed the line):** step every input that feeds the chart (a counter, a unit, a date) and print the y-axis ticks and the line's path before and after; an axis that re-fits to the data on every step, so the line always fills the plot and only the tick labels change, is a finding (pin the axis to the largest value the control can reach). Say what one step is worth in words beside the control, and mark the moment the person is waiting for (the day a line crosses a guide) on the chart as well as in the text.
    - **Every mark the chart can draw is seen in a seeded state (owner, round 18: the break-even marker never showed for the seeded member, who was already ahead, and the report called it "could not check"):** list each mark, ring, guide and divider the chart can draw and the state that draws it, and open the seeded members until each has been seen; a mark that no seeded member triggers is invisible to the owner, and a mark's look is judged with the chart (a bold ring or dot that outweighs the line, or a label that appears only on hover, is a finding: marks are small, quiet and labelled in place), so report it as a finding (give the chart a state that shows it, such as history, or seed one) and never as "not checked".
    - **Text alternative:** the chart has one that states its end value.
    - **Height and fold:** the chart is a share of the viewport (about 35% or less at 568 tall, capped on a short landscape phone), the headline and verdict stay above the fold at 320×568, and the card's top moves by no more than a line between scenarios.
    - **Settled:** shots are taken after any draw animation ends; a dot or label that arrives before its line is a finding.
