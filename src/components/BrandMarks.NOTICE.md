# Brand marks

The Claude and Gemini marks in `BrandMarks.tsx` are path data from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.37.0 (`claude`, `googlegemini`), released under CC0 1.0 and traced from claude.ai and gemini.google.com. The Gemini gradient approximates the product's own colours.

The marks themselves are trademarks of Anthropic and Google. They are used only to name the product a step or row refers to, never to suggest endorsement (Simple Icons' disclaimer: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md). The GitHub and Google Calendar marks follow the same use.
