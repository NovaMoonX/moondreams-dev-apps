# Going Once 🔨

Going Once is for selling a lot of your things when you'd rather someone else did the selling. Listing each thing yourself means answering every buyer, and a shared spreadsheet never says what is still available. Here you add each thing once, with its photos, a price, its condition and a floor price only you and your co-sellers see; a co-seller you add hears about every interested buyer, talks to them and records what sold. Buyers get one link that shows only what is still for sale, and they say they're interested through a short form, so nobody's contact details go on the page.

It is for three people: the seller, who adds things once and checks in; the co-seller, who talks to buyers, records sales and relists things on Marketplace; and the buyer, who browses the link without an account.

## How it works

1. Start a sale and add your things: photos, a name, a price and its condition.
2. Add a co-seller by email; they see everything, floor prices and notes included.
3. Turn on the public link and share it. It shows Available and Pending things, live.
4. Buyers tap "I'm interested" and the message lands in a shared inbox. You and your co-sellers get a push naming the thing, with several close together bundled as one and nothing overnight.
5. Mark things Pending when a pickup is set and Sold with what was paid.
6. Coming: copy a thing's listing text and save its photos, or save every thing's photos at once, to relist on Marketplace.
7. See what has sold against what was asked. Coming: finish the sale when it's over; buyers' names, contact details and messages are cleared 30 days later.
