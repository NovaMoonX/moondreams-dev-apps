# Going Once 🔨

> App id `going-once`. Everything below is the product vision; `UX.md` and `TECHNICAL.md` follow it. Words used everywhere: a **sale** holds **things**; the people are the **seller**, a **co-seller** and a **buyer**; a buyer's message is an **inquiry**; the private lowest acceptable price is the **floor price**.

## Who It Is For

Three people, confirmed by the owner. Every change names the person it serves and is walked as that person, and a change for one never breaks another's journey. The names match the example sale on the canvas (Fall clear-out); they are illustrations, not real people.

### The seller (Maya, the owner)

- **Situation.** Maya has a lot of things to let go of (a clear-out, a move, an estate) and dreads the back-and-forth with buyers. She knows each thing best: where it came from, what it cost, what to ask and the floor she'd take.
- **Journey they repeat.** Add each thing once from her phone (photos, a name, a price, its condition, and a floor price and notes when they matter), add a co-seller, share the public link, then check in now and then on what sold, for how much, and what came in. Finish the sale when it is over.
- **Level.** Owns the sale. Can do everything a co-seller can, and alone deletes things, manages co-sellers, turns the public link on or off or gives it a new address, edits what the public page says about the sale, and finishes, reopens or deletes the sale. Rarely marks anything sold.
- **Never wants to see.** A buyer's message she has to answer herself, a form that blocks saving until every field is filled, or the floor price and notes anywhere a buyer could see them.

### The co-seller (Theo, the friend doing the selling)

- **Situation.** Theo agreed to sell for Maya. He answers interest, texts and calls buyers, meets them at pickup, and relists most things on Facebook Marketplace, where more buyers look.
- **Journey they repeat.** Get a push that someone is interested, open the inquiry, reach the buyer, negotiate with the floor price and notes in view, mark the thing Pending when a pickup is set and Sold with what was paid. To relist, copy a thing's listing text and save its photos, or save every thing's photos at once when first listing the whole sale.
- **Level.** Added by Maya by email, signs in with his own account, removable, and can leave. Sees everything private; adds and edits things; changes statuses, prices paid and inquiries; takes a thing off the list; copies and shares the public link. Can undo his own add (a thing he made in the last 10 minutes, with no pickup or sale on it), but never deletes anything else, manages co-sellers, changes the link or the sale's details, or finishes or deletes the sale. Needs no access of his own to Going Once: being added is enough.
- **Never wants to see.** A buyer he heard about too late, a buyer he can't reach, a thing two people promised because a status lagged, photos too small to relist, or a floor price he has to ask Maya for mid-conversation.

### The buyer (Priya, who got the link)

- **Situation.** Priya saw the link in a group chat, on a board or in a Marketplace post, opens it on her phone, has no account and won't make one.
- **Journey they repeat.** Scroll what is still for sale, open a thing for its photos, price and condition, and tap "I'm interested" with a name, a way to reach her and a message.
- **Level.** Signed out, on the public page. Can look and ask about one thing at a time, never act on it.
- **Never wants to see.** Something that turns out to be long gone, a sign-up wall, or anything private: the floor price, notes, other buyers, or who is selling.

## The Short

Going Once is for selling a pile of your things without doing the selling yourself. You add each thing once, with its photos, a price, its condition and a floor price only you and your co-sellers see. You add a co-seller, and they hear about every interested buyer, talk to them, mark things Pending and Sold, and record what was paid. Anyone with the public link sees what is still for sale and can say they're interested; their message lands in an inbox the seller and co-sellers share, and a buyer's contact details never appear on the page. At any point the sale says what has sold, for how much against what was asked, and what is left, and when it is over you finish it.

## The Story

It started with a home to clear out and a seller who hates selling. A friend was happy to handle the buyers, but that meant the friend needed every detail: what each thing is, what it cost, what to ask, how low to go, and which ones were already promised. A shared spreadsheet and a camera roll don't say which things are still available, and a Marketplace listing per thing means answering every buyer yourself. Going Once keeps the list in one place for the seller and co-sellers, and gives buyers one link that only ever shows what is still for sale.

## How It Works

1. **Start a sale.** Give it a name ("Fall clear-out") and, if you like, a pickup area and a note for buyers.
2. **Add your things.** Four answers each: photos, a name, a price and its condition. Anything else is optional and can come later.
3. **Add a co-seller.** Type their email; when they sign in, the sale is waiting for them.
4. **Turn on the public link.** Share it anywhere. It shows what is Available and Pending, live, and never Sold things or anything private.
5. **Buyers say they're interested.** One short form per thing: name, phone or email, a message.
6. **Hear about it, and answer.** The seller and every co-seller get a push when someone is interested (on unless they turn it off; several close together come as one push, never dropped), reach the buyer from the inbox, and mark it Handled.
7. **Mark Pending, then Sold.** Pending tells buyers someone is picking it up; Sold takes it off the public page and records what was paid.
8. **Relist elsewhere.** Copy a thing's listing text and save its photos, or save every thing's photos at once, for Marketplace or anywhere else.
9. **See where the sale stands.** What sold and for how much against what was asked, what is pending, and what is left.
10. **Finish the sale.** The public page closes, and buyers' details are cleared 30 days later.

### Things and What Each Field Shows, In Detail

The real input is a person walking through a home with a phone: twenty to a hundred things, often over several sittings, three to six photos each, most from the camera, some already in the photo library. So adding a thing starts from its photos (several picked at once, or taken one after another, up to 12), the thing is saved from its first photo or its name, and the walk goes on to the next thing without a Save button. A thing goes on the public page only once it has a photo, a name and a price ("Free" counts as a price); until then it is "Not listed yet".

**No categories.** Things are found by status (Available, Pending, Sold) and search; nobody sorts them into kinds, so adding a thing stays four answers.

| Field | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Photos (in order; the first is the cover), up to 12 | Everyone |
| Name, description, brand or model, size | Everyone |
| Condition: New, Like new, Good, Fair, Well used | Everyone |
| Asking price, or Free | Everyone |
| Original price | Seller and co-sellers; buyers too when "Show original price" is on ("Was $400, now $150") |
| Where it was bought, and a link to the store's page ("Wayfair · Oak writing desk"), shown as a preview card | Seller and co-sellers |
| Floor price | Seller and co-sellers |
| Notes ("scratch on the left leg", "comes with the cushions") | Seller and co-sellers |
| Status, price paid, who marked it | Seller and co-sellers; buyers see only Available or Pending |

- **The floor price is a guide, not a lock.** A sold price under it is saved as typed, with one quiet line on the thing ("Sold under your floor price").
- **A thing can be taken off the list** (kept, or given away) by the seller or a co-seller without deleting it: it leaves the public page and the totals, says so on its row, and can be put back.
- **Only the seller deletes a thing**, so a co-seller never loses the seller's photos or notes. The one exception is undoing an add: a co-seller can delete a thing he made in the last 10 minutes that has no pickup or sale on it (the "Added Oak desk · Undo" line, and Delete in its More for those 10 minutes).
- **Photos are kept at listing quality**, as large as Marketplace shows, so a co-seller's relisting looks as good as the original. The public page shows smaller copies.

### Status, In Detail

One noun, three states. The same words are used on every screen, in the inbox, in pushes and on the public page.

| Status | Means | Public page |
|---|---|---|
| **Available** | For sale | Shown, with "I'm interested" |
| **Pending** | A buyer is set to pick it up | Shown as "Pending · someone's picking this up", with "I'm interested" kept as a backup ask |
| **Sold** | Gone, with what was paid | Not shown |

- **Pending can name who is picking it up** (one of its inquiries, or a typed name) and a pickup day, both optional, so a co-seller sees who is coming and when. Back to Available is one tap if the pickup falls through.
- **Sold asks for what was paid**, filled with the asking price so the usual case is one tap, and optionally how they paid (Cash, Venmo, Zelle, Other) and the day (today by default). It records who marked it.
- **Change it, or look it up.** A sold thing's price and day can be corrected (a typo), and the sheet says what the change does to the sale's total before it saves ("Collected goes from $225 to $215"). "Not sold after all" puts it back to Available and takes it out of the total, with an Undo. Each thing keeps a short history of its status changes (who and when), which is looked up, never edited.
- **Two people at once.** If Theo marks a thing Sold while Maya has it open, her screen follows the live status; a status change made from a stale screen is refused in words ("Theo already marked this sold for $170").

### Interest and the Inbox, In Detail

- **The buyer's form** asks for a name, a phone number or an email (at least one), and a message, about one thing. It sends with no account and says, once sent, "Sent. The seller will reach out to you." It never promises when and never names anyone. A buyer can ask about a Pending thing too, and the form says someone is picking it up. A thing that sold while the buyer was typing refuses the form in words ("This one just sold, sorry") and keeps what she typed.
- **A push when someone is interested, on by default.** The seller and every co-seller get one, because a buyer heard about late is a buyer lost. It is never dropped and never floods:
  - **Bundled.** At most one push per person every 15 minutes. One new inquiry reads "New interest in Gray sofa"; several read "3 new inquiries · Gray sofa and 2 more".
  - **Quiet at night.** Nothing from 10 PM to 8 AM local; what came in overnight arrives as one push at 8 AM.
  - **Private on a lock screen.** It names the thing and the sale, never the buyer's name, phone, email, message or a price.
  - **Opens what it is about.** One inquiry opens that inquiry; a bundle opens the inbox on New.
- **The phone's permission is asked at the moment it matters**: the first time the person opens the inbox, or right after joining as a co-seller, through the site's own push flow, never on load. After "Not now" it comes back on the inbox, at most three times. A switch in the sale's settings ("Push me when someone's interested") turns it off for that person and that sale.
- **The inbox** is shared by the seller and every co-seller: newest first, each inquiry with the thing's photo and name, the buyer's name and message, and buttons to call, text or email them (the reply happens in the phone's own app, never in Going Once). An inquiry is New until someone marks it Handled by hand, which records who; nothing marks it for them, so an unreached buyer never drops out of New.
- **A thing's sheet lists its inquiries**, so a co-seller choosing who gets it sees everyone who asked, in order.
- **Buyer contact details are private** to the seller and co-sellers, never on the public page, in a push or in copied listing text.
- **A thing that is Sold** keeps its inquiries for the record, each marked "(Sold)" in the inbox; the form on the public page is gone with it.

### Sharing and Access, In Detail

| Who | How they get in | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Seller | Made the sale | Everything: add, edit and delete things; manage co-sellers; turn the public link on or off and give it a new address; edit the sale's name, pickup area and note; finish, reopen or delete the sale |
| Co-seller | Seller added their email; they sign in | Read everything; add and edit things; undo their own add within 10 minutes; change statuses, prices paid and inquiries; take things off the list and put them back; copy, open and share the public link; save photos; leave the sale. Not delete anything else, manage co-sellers, change the link or the sale's details, or finish, reopen or delete the sale |
| Removed co-seller | Seller removed them, or they left | Nothing from that moment; their past marks keep their name |
| Anyone with the public link | Opens it, no account | See Available and Pending things with their public fields; send an interest form |

- **Starting a sale is a feature switch** (`create-sales`), open to everyone signed in by default. A person kept out of it still opens and works every sale they were added to, so a co-seller never needs access of his own.
- **The seller owns the sale's exposure.** Whether the page is on, its address, and what it says about the sale are the seller's alone; a co-seller spreads the link but never changes who can reach it.
- **The public page is live, and says so.** It always shows the current statuses; it is not a snapshot. A new address stops the old one at once; turning the link off makes the page say the sale isn't here, to everyone with it.
- **The seller and co-sellers see it marked as theirs.** Opening the page while signed in as either shows one line, "This is your sale's public page", with the way back into the app. Nobody else can tell whose it is.
- **It never says who is selling.** It shows the sale's name, pickup area and note as the seller typed them, and the name field reminds the seller not to put their own name or address there.
- **Previews say less.** A link pasted into a chat shows a generic "Things for sale" preview, never the sale's name or a thing's photo.
- **A pickup area, never an address.** The sale asks for an area; the street address is shared by the co-seller once a pickup is set.

### Selling and Relisting (the co-seller's journey), In Detail

This is Theo's loop, and every tool in it is one tap from where he is.

1. **An inquiry comes in.** A push names the thing; tapping it opens the inquiry with the buyer's name, message, and Call, Text and Email buttons.
2. **Negotiate with the facts in view.** Opening a thing (from the inbox or the list) shows, beside the public details, a private part only the seller and co-sellers see: floor price, original price, where it was bought and Maya's notes. Nothing private needs a second tap or a question to Maya.
3. **A pickup is set.** Mark Pending, naming who and the day if it helps.
4. **It's picked up.** Mark Sold: the price is filled with the asking price, so the usual case is one tap, with how they paid optional.
5. **Relist one thing.** Copy listing text, then Save photos, and paste and attach on Marketplace.
6. **Relist the whole sale.** Save all photos once, then copy each thing's text as it is listed.

- **Copy listing text** (seller and co-sellers) copies the thing's name, condition, price and description in a plain form ready to paste into Marketplace, with "Was $400" only when Show original price is on. It never includes the floor price, notes, where it was bought or any buyer's details, and the button says what it copies.
- **Save photos (one thing)** sends that thing's photos at listing quality to the phone's share sheet, to save to the camera roll, and downloads them where the browser has no share sheet. This is the way into the camera roll.
- **Save all photos (the whole sale)** makes one zip of every Available and Pending thing's photos at listing quality, one folder per thing named after it, so each set matches its listing. Before starting it says how many things and photos it holds and about how large it is, and that it is best on Wi-Fi. It lands in the phone's Files or a computer's downloads, not the camera roll; Sold, taken-off and not-yet-listed things are left out.
- **The app remembers what was relisted.** Copying a thing's listing text records who copied it and when, keeping only the most recent copy ("Theo copied this listing on Oct 28"; if Maya copies it later, the line names Maya and her day instead), shown on the thing, so whoever marks it Sold is reminded to take the other listing down (below).
- Going Once doesn't post to Marketplace or track listings there.

### Where the Sale Stands, In Detail

- **Collected:** the sum of what was paid for Sold things. **Asked for those:** their asking prices beside it, so "$225 of $245 asked" reads at a glance.
- **Pending:** a count and what they are asking.
- **Left:** Available things and what they are asking. Things with no price yet are counted, not priced ("1 has no price yet"), and taken-off things are left out of every figure.
- The floor price is never summed; it is a per-thing guide, not a target.

### Finishing a Sale, In Detail

A sale ends when the seller says so, not on a date.

- **Finish the sale** (Maya only, at the end of the sale's settings) asks once, saying what it does: the public page closes for everyone with the link, pushes stop, and buyers' details are cleared 30 days from now. It names what is left ("7 things didn't sell. 2 are still pending: Oak desk for Nate on Sat, 27" monitor for Dev") so nothing is finished by surprise.
- **What didn't sell stays.** Available things stay as they were, listed under "Didn't sell" with what they were asking, ready to keep, give away or carry into a later sale (Next Steps). Nothing is deleted.
- **A pickup can still happen.** A Pending thing on a finished sale can still be marked Sold or Not sold after all, so Saturday's pickup is recorded. What a finished sale no longer offers is anything that would find a new buyer: Mark pending, the public page, the interest form and Save all photos.
- **A finished sale reads as finished.** It moves under "Finished" in each person's sales, its figures stay, and a line says when buyers' details will be cleared ("Buyer details are cleared on Dec 2"), then that they were.
- **What clearing takes.** On the 30th day every inquiry's name, phone, email and message are blanked, and so are the buyer names on Pending and Sold records, because a message often holds a number. What stays is the thing, the day it was sent, who handled it, and every price paid: "A buyer asked about Gray sofa · Oct 30".
- **Changing your mind.** Within those 30 days Maya can reopen the sale: everything is kept and the clearing is called off. The public page stays off until she turns it on again. After the clearing, a reopened sale stays cleared, and the reopen confirm says so.
- **Co-sellers** see the sale as finished and keep read access, so a late question ("what did the bike go for?") is still answered.

### When Something Goes Sideways, In Detail

The edge journeys, each walked as the person it happens to.

- **Maya removes Theo mid-sale.** The confirm says what happens: "Theo loses access now. What he marked stays, with his name: 2 pickups he set up are still Pending." He loses access at once and his pushes stop. Every hold, sale and Handled mark he made keeps his name; nothing reverts. If Theo had the sale open, his next action is refused and his screen says "You're no longer on Fall clear-out" with a way back to his own sales. Things he added are the sale's and stay. (Cash he collected is tracked by "who holds the money", Next Steps.)
- **Maya deletes a thing that has a pickup set.** The confirm names what goes with it: "Nate is set to pick this up Sat. 3 people asked about it." and offers **Take it off the list instead** as the gentler way first. Deleting a Sold thing says what it does to the total ("Collected goes from $225 to $55"). The thing's inquiries stay in the inbox under the name it had, marked "(No longer listed)", so Theo can still tell those buyers. Its photos and public copies go.
- **Priya sends again.** The same phone or email about the same thing updates the one inquiry: her new message replaces the old, it moves to the top of New, and the inbox shows "Sent 2 times". If someone had marked it Handled, it is New again and pushes as new; if it was still New, no second push. Priya sees the same Sent confirmation either way. About a different thing, it is a separate inquiry.
- **A thing sells on Marketplace.** Theo marks it Sold here like any other, with "Other" under how they paid when it fits. Inquiries about it here then read "(Sold)" in the inbox so he can tell those buyers. The other way round: when a thing whose listing text was copied is marked Sold here, the confirmation names only whoever copied it most recently ("Theo copied this listing on Oct 28. Take that listing down too.") Going Once can't see Marketplace, so it reminds rather than promises.
- **The sale is finished with things unsold.** As in Finishing a Sale: the confirm names what didn't sell and what is still pending, nothing is deleted, pending pickups can still be recorded, and a later sale can start from what is left (Next Steps).

## What Makes It Different

It is not a marketplace and not a listing site. The seller is not the one selling, so the app is built around a hand-off: private fields that travel to the co-seller but never to a buyer, a shared inbox and a push instead of a public contact, and statuses that keep two people from promising one thing twice. Buyers never make an account and never pay through the app. That changes what gets built: two audiences on every thing (field by field), co-sellers rather than viewers, a relisting path out to Marketplace rather than a competing feed, and a sale that ends and forgets its buyers' details.

## How It Feels

- **Quick to fill.** Four answers per thing, saved from the first photo; the walk through a room never stops for a form.
- **Handed off.** After adding things and a co-seller, the seller can stop thinking about it and still know what sold.
- **Never late.** A buyer's interest reaches someone's phone the moment it is sent.
- **Always current.** A buyer never asks about something that sold yesterday; a co-seller never sells something already promised.
- **Discreet.** The public page never names the seller, shows contact details or hints at the floor price, and a finished sale lets buyers' details go.
- **Plain.** Counts and dollars, no hype, no urgency tricks ("Only 2 left!").
- **Phone first.** Built for a thumb, in a room, with things in front of you.

## Under the Hood

- **Stack:** React with Redux Toolkit for state, Firestore for sales, things and inquiries, Storage for photos, Cloud Functions for the public page, the interest form, the push, the photo zip and the clearing, Dreamer UI for components.
- **Data:** A sale is a document with its owner and co-sellers as members; things and inquiries live under it. Money is whole cents. A sold day and a pickup day are dates with no time; status changes, inquiries, a copied listing and a sale's finish are instants.
- **Two audiences:** What a buyer may see is copied out by name into what the public page serves; private fields (floor price, notes, original price unless switched on, where bought, buyer details) never leave the seller's and co-sellers' reach.
- **Public page:** An unguessable link read through a function that returns only the allowed fields of Available and Pending things, with smaller photo copies in a folder named by the link; not indexed, never says who shared it, loads nothing third-party.
- **Interest form:** Written by a function so signed-out buyers can send it, with caps on size and how often one visitor can send; a resend from the same contact about the same thing updates one inquiry.
- **Push:** Sent through the site's push service, bundled per person to one per 15 minutes and held to one 8 AM push overnight; never dropped, and never carrying a buyer's details.
- **Photos:** Shrunk on the device to listing size (2,048px JPEG), which is what Marketplace shows; the public page gets smaller copies; Save all photos is a zip built by a function.
- **Finishing:** A scheduled function clears buyers' names, contact and messages 30 days after a sale is finished, unless it was reopened, and leaves the thing, the day and every price.
