# Going Once and Nomos

**In one line:** ask Nomos what is still for sale, what is waiting for pickup, and who bought or asked about what.

## What Nomos can read

Pickups, open sales, your things (On); all things, buyer questions (Off, read when asked); **Buyers and co-sellers** (person, Off): a buyer's first name and the sale they asked in, a co-seller's first name and the sales you share.

## Bringing them into the circle

Buyers and co-sellers can be brought in as people. A first name beside one fuller name in the circle ("Sam" and "Sam Lee") is asked, never merged unasked; a co-seller who is already there keeps their record and gains the line that they sell with you. See `.claude/rules/helm.md` "Bring in what an app holds".

## Not shared

Buyers' emails, phone numbers, messages and full names, where things were bought, floor prices, private notes.

## Keeping it current

A new stored record of a person or business in this app is a category and an entry in Helm's `BRING_IN_SOURCES`.
