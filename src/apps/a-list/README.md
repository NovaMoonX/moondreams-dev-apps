# A-List Tracker 🎟️

A-List Tracker is a phone-first companion for an AMC Stubs A-List membership. The membership is a flat monthly fee, so its value only shows up over time, and only if you remember what each ticket would have cost, including the upcharge for IMAX or Dolby. The app answers the question every member eventually asks: is this membership paying for itself? Each movie night shows as a poster on a calendar, and your savings and break-even status sit one glance away.

## How it works

1. Set up your membership: your monthly cost, the total on your bill with tax, and the day you started.
2. Add the movies you have already seen, so your savings start accurate.
3. Keep a watchlist with a priority and a preferred format for each movie.
4. Put movies on the calendar, watched or planned, and tag the theater.
5. Mark a movie paid with its ticket price, fee and tax, and see what you saved against a standard ticket.
6. After a planned showing ends, say whether you saw it and leave a star rating.
7. Share a week, a month or any dates with a friend through a link they can open without an account.
