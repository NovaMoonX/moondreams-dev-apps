# Nine Lives

Nine Lives is a private place for cat owners to keep track of what matters about their cats: who they are, what has happened to them, what is coming up, and what it all costs. It replaces scattered vet paperwork, phone photos of vaccine cards and half-remembered symptom timelines with one record, organized around your household and each cat in it. It was started after adopting two kittens and finding how much there is to keep straight from day one.

## How it works

1. Create a household, or join one with an invite code and wait for approval.
2. Add each cat with a name, photo, birth date, breed, microchip number and vet clinic.
3. Build a health record: upload documents, log vet visits, conditions, symptoms and preventive doses.
4. Track what it costs, from adoption fees to food, litter and vet bills.
5. Check the household timeline for visits and due dates, past and coming up.
6. Let the app fill in entries from a vet document or a spoken note, then review them before saving.
