# Waypoint and Nomos

**In one line:** ask Nomos what is on tomorrow's itinerary, what you owe the group, and who you travel with.

## What Nomos can read

Trips, itinerary, my to-dos (On); what you owe and are owed, trip checklist, full checklist, ideas, announcements (Off); **People you travel with** (person, Off): each fellow traveller's name and the trips you share. A name that is the person's email is left out.

## Bringing them into the circle

Fellow travellers can be brought into the circle as people; one who matches someone already there (a contact, a co-seller) is merged and keeps both records' facts. See `.claude/rules/helm.md` "Bring in what an app holds".

## Not shared

Other members' private to-dos, contact details, timeline suggestions, expense lines, booking confirmation codes, receipts, the album.

## Keeping it current

A new stored record of a person or business in this app is a category and an entry in Helm's `BRING_IN_SOURCES`.
