# Helm UX canvas: board specs

The lead publishes these as the **Helm UX** Design artifact. Boards are 390 x 844 (status bar 44, header 60 on home, a 52px BackLink bar plus the page title on views, the dock 124 incl. safe area, about 616 for content on home). There is no tab bar and no Tell pill (round 6): the voice dock (the owner's pick) carries Talk (a 68px ink circle, labelled, centred) with Browse and Type as 48px outlined circles 28px either side, on a fade from the page colour; while listening the sides are Cancel and Attach; while thinking or speaking Talk becomes Stop alone. **Round 6e:** the dock is home's only; every content page ends in the **ask bar** instead: an edge-to-edge `card` strip with a 1px `border` top hairline, 92px tall (padding 10 12 34 for the home indicator), holding one 48px row in `secondary`, radius 14: {Name}'s mark (30, on a `card` disc), the label 15/600 `foreground` with an ellipsis, then a mic and a keyboard as 40px icon buttons in `muted-foreground`. Content pages scroll above it (main ends at 92 from the bottom); it never moves. A content page's app bar is 52px: BackLink (where you came from), then a 40px Home icon button (left out when Back reads "Home") and ⋯. A board that overflows is clipped. No emoji in the design except the person's own (project emoji).

**`{Name}`** is the Assistant name Tweak (default Nomos). Every board renders it; the mark shows its first letter. Nothing on any board says "Nomos" except through `{Name}`.

## Look and tokens

"The household's private secretary": cool mist paper, deep blue-teal ink, one restrained cool accent (verdigris), serif titles over the site's sans. Its own palette, set apart from every other app (Going Once is cream and forest green, Runway and Waypoint blue). No nautical words. All text pairs below measure at least 4.5:1 (checked; lowest is muted text on the page at 5.6:1 light).

**Themes.** The default is **Chestnut & Sand** (warm paper, brown ink, primary #7A5434 light / #E0BF9C dark); the person can pick Plum & Pewter, Mist & Deep Teal or Porcelain & Indigo in Settings > Colours. **Type.** Titles in a serif: **Source Serif 4** 600 (OFL, self-hosted, Latin subset; fallback `"Iowan Old Style", Charter, Georgia, serif`). Page title 28/34 serif 600, letter-spacing -0.01em; sheet title and identity name 22/28 serif 600; section 15/600 sans; row title 15/500; body 15/400; meta 13/400 muted. Figures tabular. Sentence case; no eyebrows.

**Shapes.** Card 14, field 10, pill full, ticks round (20px, 40px hit), mark disc 22px. Hairlines; no card shadows. Icons lucide 18px, 1.75 stroke.

**App logo** (`public/logos/by-app/logo-helm.svg`, 1024 square): ink navy #1D2B4C tile; a 1px-at-64 silver (#C3CEE4) hairline frame inset 112px with 48px corners, like a letterhead border; centred in it a serif capital H (Source Serif 4 600 outlined to paths) in silver, its crossbar drawn as two fine parallel rules. Reads as a monogram on stationery; no wheel, no anchor.

**Round 3: no new tokens.** The general-knowledge card is `card` with a `border` hairline and a muted-foreground globe heading, set against the records card's `secondary` fill, so the two parts differ by fill, edge, icon and a labelled divider, never by colour alone. The health caution block is `muted` fill with ink 13/600 text. A note's source mark is lucide `BookMarked` 14px in the source line's colour. Where the mark sits on the `secondary` result card its disc takes `card`, so it stays visible.

**Round 6: no new tokens.** The reply's large type is 22/30 (listening 24/32) sans 500 in ink, the not-yet-spoken or unsettled tail in muted-foreground. Photos on the boards are drawn as soft tonal gradients in the palette's greys (a who's photo a 64px circle on its page, 36px in rows; a place's 4:3 strip), never stock faces. A who without a photo keeps the initial disc; a pet without one shows lucide `PawPrint`, an organization `Building`. New lucide icons: `LayoutGrid` (Browse), `Keyboard` (Type), `Paperclip` (Attach), `Square` (Stop), `Volume2`, `Ear`, `Camera`, `Image`, `MapPin`, `Users`, `History` (Activity), `Repeat` (procedures), `ArrowUp` (Send), `PawPrint`, `Building`, and after the review `Cake` (key dates).

**Round 6d: no new tokens.** What the person sent is a right-aligned bubble in `primary` with `primary-foreground` 15/21, radius 18 18 4 18, max 84% wide, its time 13 muted under it (bubbles of one turn stack 4 apart and share one time). {Name}'s words sit beside the 24px mark at 20/28 sans 500 (17/25 inside a sheet). The `DidCard` is `card` with a hairline, indented 34 under the words: 48px rows (icon 18 muted, title 15, meta 13 muted, chevron), a 48px foot with "Undo" (small outline) left and the time right. The context pill is `secondary` 40 tall, radius 20, 15/600 `secondary-foreground`, a 28px face or a 16px mic, ✕ 16 in a 44px hit area, centred 10 above the dock's circles. A topic document uses the page's type: headings 17/600, lists and paragraphs 15/23, the source ring 8px hollow muted after a part, a highlighted part in `accent` with a 6 radius.

**Assistant mark.** A 22px disc in secondary with the name's first letter in the serif, 12/600, secondary-foreground ("N" for Nomos, "E" for Ellery, "B" for Bartholomew). A name starting with a digit or symbol shows lucide `Feather` 12px instead. Used at the start of every assistant line, on a suggested card's top-left and on Activity rows.
