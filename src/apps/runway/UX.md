# Runway UX

How Runway is laid out and how a person moves through it. The product vision is in `README.md`. The screens themselves are drawn on the **Runway UX** canvas (an artifact); this file holds what a picture cannot: the sitemap, the rules behind each screen, the component list, the journeys and the form fields.

The canvas has one clickable screen set (Home, Days, Plan, Spend) with a Tweak that switches the level between Plan, Track and Link, plus static screens grouped by journey.

---

## 1. What is specific to Runway

| Rule | What it means on screen |
|---|---|
| The level decides the wording | An envelope figure reads *assigned* at Plan, *left* at Track and *safe to spend* at Link. Never one word for all three. |
| Emoji is optional on anything that appears as a line | Envelopes, bills and goals can each have one. It shows in a small badge on Home tiles and draft lines; with none set, the badge shows the name's first letter. |
| Hidden, not disabled | At Plan there is no Spend tab, no Add purchase, no Needs a home. They appear, with their data, when tracking is on. |
| A range is always labelled | A figure built on a range says so ("planned at the low end, $140"). |
| Nothing moves until you confirm | A draft is a suggestion. A change shows its effect on each upcoming income day before it applies. |
| Over is shown, never blocked | A purchase can push an envelope below zero. The envelope shows how far over, and Free to spend drops by the same amount. |

## 2. Sitemap

```
Sign in
 └─ Plan has an income source?
     ├─ No  → Starting Out → Home
     └─ Yes → Home

Bottom tabs        Home · Days · Plan · Spend (Track and Link only)

Home
 ├─ Free to spend, in detail
 ├─ Income day (next)
 ├─ Envelope
 ├─ Check an amount            Track and Link
 ├─ Needs a home               Track and Link, only when something waits
 └─ Where your cash is         Link

Days
 ├─ Income day
 │   ├─ Confirm money arrived (sheet)
 │   │   └─ Checklist: bills to pay, money to move
 │   ├─ Planned and confirmed (record)
 │   └─ Correct a confirmed day
 └─ Expected (expense or income) → the Bill or Income form (proposals: later)

Plan  (pills: Income · Bills · Envelopes · Goals; a pill is drawn once its screen exists)
 ├─ Income source → Pay line
 ├─ Bill
 ├─ Envelope → Purchases (Track and Link) · History
 ├─ Goal
 └─ More
     ├─ Change log → Planned and confirmed
     ├─ Settings → Tracking and linking → Where is your money now?
     │   └─ Delete your plan (typed phrase, destructive confirm, back to first run)
     ├─ Priorities          later
     ├─ What if             later
     ├─ Accounts            later
     └─ Plan as of a date   later

Any item → Change (from when, effect) → Cover it

Spend  (Track and Link)
 ├─ Purchase → notes, links, receipts, group
 ├─ Add purchase
 ├─ Import a CSV
 ├─ Needs a home
 ├─ Card reserve
 └─ Check a balance
```

Home, Days and Plan are always present. Everything not a tab is a full screen or a sheet over the screen it came from.

**Not yet drawn:** the income source and pay line detail screens, bill and goal detail screens, Priorities, Accounts, Plan as of a date, Check a balance, Card reserve, Import a CSV, Where your cash is, Cover it as its own screen, Notification settings (four switches with a one-line explanation each, Next Steps). They follow the same patterns as the drawn ones.

**Where this screen set stops.** Envelopes, bills and income days stay in the tens. Purchases and history do not, so Spend and the change log load one date window at a time and search covers the window in view, not all time (encrypted fields cannot be searched on the server). *As built:* the change log has Older and Newer windows; Spend and Days list the months since the newest monthly closing (every figure still counts older purchases), and a window for older purchases and income days is not built yet, so an older purchase cannot be opened, edited or picked as a refund's original.

**Updating your history (Issue 28).** Figures from earlier months are added up once a month and kept as closings, so a plan opens on the months since the newest one, however old it is. The one thing a person sees of it is a card, "Updating your history", drawn in place of every screen while closings are rebuilt: the first open of a plan with months behind it (about a minute at four times slower than a laptop for five years, once), after a purchase, income day or payment in an earlier month was changed, or when two months have passed unopened. It says that nothing is changed or lost and is one card with no button, so no figure from before the change is ever drawn beside it. When it cannot finish (offline), the plan opens on what it has, or says "Your history could not be updated right now" with "Try again" when it has nothing. Spend and Days then list the months since the newest closing, and Spend says so ("Showing purchases from Oct 1. Earlier purchases are counted in your figures but not listed."); "Where is your money now?" asks for a day inside that window.

## 5. User Journeys

Arrows read left to right. A branch is in parentheses.

1. **First session.** (Sign in, or start signed out and sign in at the end) → add an income source (schedule, amount, next date) → add a bill → add an envelope → add a goal → say where things stand (or "Later") → Home with a draft for the next income day. Only the income source is required. Coming back part-way asks "Continue where you left off" or "Start over".
2. **Payday.** Home → next income day → read the draft → (change a line for this day) → Confirm money arrived → enter amount and date → Confirmed → checklist → tick bills and transfers → Done.
3. **The money was not what was planned.** Confirm → enter the real amount → (same: confirm) → difference shown → Free to spend by default, or edit the draft lines → add a note → Confirm → History keeps planned and confirmed.
4. **Change the plan.** Plan → open Groceries → Change → new amount → from when → effect on each upcoming day → (a day is short: Top up or Cover it, pick what gives) → Apply change → change log entry with an optional note.
5. **Add a purchase** (Track and Link). Envelope or Spend → Add → "How much was it?" (the large figure, keyboard up) → Next → "What was it for?" (one of the five used most, or More; skipped when opened from an envelope; tapping one moves on by itself) → the folded answers with the day (today) → Add → (over the envelope: it shows how far over, Free to spend drops, Cover it is offered).
6. **Link an account** (leaves the app). Settings → Tracking and linking → Link → explainer (read-only, sign in at the bank) → the bank's own sign-in in a new tab → (not approved: back in Runway, nothing linked) → pick a start date → Where is your money now? → hand-entered purchases offered as matches → Linked.
7. **Move down a level.** Settings → Tracking and linking → (unlink an account: new purchases stop, history stays) or (stop tracking: purchase screens hide, history stays) → figures change from *safe to spend* to *left*, or to *assigned* → recorded in the change log.
8. **An expected expense or income.** (As built the form saves the bill or income at once; the proposal step is later.) Days → Add expected money → expense or income → name, amount or range, start date, repeats (and ends, if it repeats) → proposed changes for each income day it touches, with the total and the last date → (Edit) / Apply / Not now → applied changes appear in the change log as ordinary changes.

Notes on the table:
- "Amount" is always `AmountField` drawn as the hero figure (`isHero`): exact by default, "It varies" under it for a range. A range shows both ends and the plan's stance ("Planned at the low end"), and "Plan it at one end" opens the stance pills.
- *As built (redesign):* every create form is a `QuestionFlow`. Bill: name, how much, when it is due (no day picked to start; picking one sets Repeats to Monthly, Once for an expected expense) and whether it repeats, then folded: ends (never), paid from (spread over income days), priority (Should), emoji (none). Envelope: name, how much each time (with "Every income day ›" under the figure), then folded: funded, priority, emoji. Goal: name, target, by when (empty for no date), how much each time (prefilled from the target and the date, with the pace it gives), then folded: funded, priority, emoji. Income: where it comes from, take-home pay, how often and the next payday, then folded: ends, what the pay is called (Paycheck). The pinned footer's primary is Next until every answer has been seen, then Add. Purchase: how much, what it was for, then folded: the day (today), with "Add a place" and "Add a note" chips. *As built (round 3):* a question answered by one pick (a purchase's envelope, priority, how often an envelope or goal gets money, a bill's or pay's ending, a bill's repeat once its date is in, an income source) moves on as it is picked; a pick that reveals more to fill (Chosen days, a date ending) waits for Next. A field a chip reveals takes focus. Cancel or Discard on a form opened from an item's sheet returns to that sheet. "Where things stand" asks Keep editing / Discard like the other forms. A change opened at its amount has no "Every income day ›" link under the figure, since its Funded row is right below. A change opened from an income day shows "From when" open with "This income day only" and "From this income day on" first; elsewhere it is folded to "From now on ›". An envelope's change says which pairing sets it on its income days and no longer sends the person to another tab. *As built (round 4, Starting out):* income asks "How much lands?" with "How often it arrives comes next" under the figure; "It varies" splits it and "The same each time" takes it back; "Choose which end to plan for" asks one question, "Which end should the plan count on?", with the end the plan already uses marked "like your plan" and a help icon saying what planning at one end does. Twice a month asks two days of the month as selects (1st to 30th and "Last day"), not a date. A bill's name has quick picks (Rent, Mortgage, Electricity, Water, Internet, Phone, Car payment, Car insurance, Health insurance, Streaming, Gym) that fill the name and an emoji, and an envelope's has Groceries, Dining out, Gas, Household and Fun money.
- "Priority" is Must, Should or Want on a bill, envelope or goal. "Funding rule" is when an envelope receives money. They are different fields and never share a row.
- No entity has an initial set long enough to need steps or `Disclosure`, apart from the later tax and deduction list.
- Priority is asked on the form of a bill, envelope or goal, and starts at Should so a quick add never has to answer it.

## Round 4, package C: Home, Days, Plan and Spend

- **Warnings.** The problems row on Home and Days, the rows it opens and a day's "The plan leaves Rent $30 short" line use the warning tint, edge and icon (`--runway-warning-*` in `runway.css`), never the app's blue.
- **Things to fix.** One row per problem, soonest first: "Rent $30 short" over "On Nov 2", an icon chip and a chevron; a bill past due or with no income reads "Due Nov 1". The counted row above Free to spend names the first of the same list and counts the rest. The list is open while `?fixes=true` is in the URL, so Back, Escape and the header's back from a row's income day land on the open list, and one more Back closes it. The engine reports the first short line of each income day, so a day with several short lines shows one row.
- **Screens opened from an income day.** Check an amount sits above the day (Back returns to it); an item's History and Purchases are views inside its sheet, so the day stays where it was.
- **Days.** The headline, its help icon and "Add expected" share one row; below 390px the action is a calendar icon named "Add expected money".
- **Plan.** Income, Bills, Envelopes and Goals are the four view pills, each with an emoji (dropped below 360px). Settings and tools (Settings, Change log) is a tile under the section, not a pill.
- **Spend.** One framed "Period" row (a drawer on a phone, a popover from `sm`) picks the period: quick periods, then older months that can hold purchases; "Spent" sits under it as the figure for the rows shown; `?period=` keeps it.
- **Calls to action** carry no "+": "Add income", "Add a bill", "Add an envelope", "Add a goal", "Add a purchase", "Add expected".
- **See all** sits on Home's Envelopes header line; "Show N more" on Days is a 14px muted row.

## Round 4, package D: private amounts

- The eye is drawn only where something masked is on the surface (a form's own answers and Starting out draw none; a sheet or subview gets it once a masked figure is in it, such as an effect preview). Each tab's title line ends in one eye button (Home, Days, Plan, Spend, same place); it shows or hides amounts on every tab. Amounts start hidden on a device, and hide again after five minutes in the background.
- Masked: every figure on every surface (tab fronts, item sheets, a goal's "of $1,800", the day screens and their rows, Things to fix, the envelope sheet, Check an amount's result, Purchases, the change log and the record of a confirmed day), and every line that gives one away. Names, dates and counts stay readable. Never masked: the figure being typed and a form's own answers (the add and change forms, Confirm money arrived, Correct a day, Starting out's steps). Every sheet, screen or subview that draws a masked figure has the eye on its title line.
- A goal's sheet offers "Change how often" (while it is being added to) or "Start adding to it" (when it is not); both open the goal's "How often" question. A calendar envelope's sheet labels its figure by its cadence ("Monthly").
- "Things to fix" lists every short line of an income day as its own row, soonest first, the higher priority first within a day.

## Round 4, package G4: floating adds, Free to spend, filters, warnings, goal chart
