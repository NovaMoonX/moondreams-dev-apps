# Runway UX

How Runway is laid out and how a person moves through it. The product vision is in `README.md`. The screens themselves are drawn on the **Runway UX** canvas (an artifact); this file holds what a picture cannot: the sitemap, the rules behind each screen, the component list, the journeys and the form fields.

The canvas has one clickable screen set (Home, Days, Plan, Spend) with a Tweak that switches the level between Plan, Track and Link, plus static screens grouped by journey. The app as built has Plan and Track only; Link is drawn for the plan.

Horizon (Plan > 🗓️ Horizon) has its own canvas, "Runway UX: Horizon": https://claude.ai/artifact/8NrXzdaLvjj9B4GAMDSXiN (see "Horizon canvas" below).

---

## 1. What is specific to Runway

| Rule                                                 | What it means on screen                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The level decides the wording                        | An envelope figure reads _assigned_ at Plan, _left_ at Track and _safe to spend_ at Link. Never one word for all three. _As built:_ Plan and Track only; Link and its wording are not built yet. |
| Emoji is optional on anything that appears as a line | Envelopes, bills and goals can each have one. It shows in a small badge on Home tiles and draft lines; with none set, the badge shows the name's first letter.                                   |
| Hidden, not disabled                                 | At Plan there is no Spend tab, no Add purchase, no Needs a home. They appear, with their data, when tracking is on.                                                                              |
| A range is always labelled                           | A figure built on a range says so ("planned at the low end, $140").                                                                                                                              |
| Nothing moves until you confirm                      | A draft is a suggestion. A change shows its effect on each upcoming income day before it applies.                                                                                                |
| Over is shown, never blocked                         | A purchase can push an envelope below zero. The envelope shows how far over, and Free to Spend drops by the same amount.                                                                         |
| How far the money goes is a sentence | An item never shows a bar or a runway chart: it says which costs its money covers, in date order, and what happens after ("Covers Movers, then Storage on Feb 1st and Mar 1st. From Apr 1st, Storage comes from your pays."). A range counts at the stance's end, and the help icon says what the other end would cover. |
| A cost is not a bill until the person makes it one | Costs live inside their item. A repeating cost offers "Make it a bill" and nothing else turns it into one. A cost paid outside the plan is listed with its date, asks no pay for anything and raises no warning, and is done only when the person marks it paid. |
| Only a range asks what it came to | Paying an exact cost is one tap, at both levels, with Undo where it was tapped (as ticking an exact bill is today). A range asks the bill's own question, "What did Movers come to?", empty with "It varies, usually $600 to $800" as the hint. Bills' own Mark paid is not changed. |
| Dates read with an ordinal | Every date Runway shows keeps its short month and adds an ordinal: "Feb 1st", "Mar 31st", "Dec 2nd". Horizon's copy is written this way from the start; the rest of the app moves onto it with Issue 90. |

## 2. Sitemap

```
Sign in
 └─ Plan has an income source?
     ├─ No  → How do you want to start? → Start from your budget → review → the step after the last kind it filled
     │                                  → Start fresh → Starting Out → Home
     └─ Yes → Home

Bottom tabs        Home · Days · Plan · Spend (Track and Link only)

Home
 ├─ Free to Spend, in detail
 ├─ Income day (next)
 ├─ Envelope
 ├─ Check an amount            Track and Link
 ├─ Needs a home               Track and Link, only when something waits
 └─ Where your cash is         Link

Days
 ├─ Income day
 │   ├─ Confirm money arrived (sheet)
 │   │   └─ Checklist: bills to pay, money to move
 │   ├─ Planned and confirmed (record)
 │   └─ Correct a confirmed day
 └─ Expected (a bill or one-time cost, money coming in, or something with several costs) → the Bill, Income or Add to your horizon form (proposals: later)

Plan  (pills: Income · Bills · Envelopes · Goals · Horizon · Cards (Track); a pill is drawn once its screen exists)
 ├─ Income source → Pay line
 ├─ Bill
 ├─ Envelope → Purchases (Track and Link) · History
 ├─ Goal
 ├─ Horizon
 │   ├─ Add to your horizon (question flow)
 │   │   └─ Add a cost (question flow, over the event's flow)
 │   ├─ Item (Moving)
 │   │   ├─ Cost (Movers)
 │   │   │   ├─ Set the price          (exact amount or still a range, its effect, why)
 │   │   │   ├─ Add what you paid      Track only: the purchase form, pointed at Movers · Moving
 │   │   │   ├─ Mark paid              both levels: exact = one tap (Undo); range = "What did Movers come to?"
 │   │   │   ├─ Paid Jan 15th (row)    once paid: a small sheet with "Take it back"
 │   │   │   ├─ Make it a bill         repeating costs only: the bill form, filled in
 │   │   │   └─ Change · Remove this cost   (Remove is hidden on a one-time cost once it is paid, with "It's paid, so it stays in the record." in its place; a repeating cost keeps Remove after payments, which ends it after today; on the last cost, Call it off instead)
 │   │   ├─ Add a cost
 │   │   ├─ Put more toward it         (from Free to Spend, an envelope or a goal, or from outside the plan)
 │   │   ├─ Change                     (name, emoji, date, how the rest is paid, priority)
 │   │   ├─ History
 │   │   ├─ Call it off                while a cost is open (where what's left goes)
 │   │   └─ Finish Moving              footer primary once every cost is paid (outside ones marked too) or a bill
 │   └─ Done (folded row) → a done item, read only; a called-off one offers Bring it back
 └─ More
     ├─ Priorities → Reorder → Save this order?
     ├─ Change log → Planned and confirmed
     ├─ Settings → Tracking and linking → Where is your money now?
     │   └─ Delete your plan (typed phrase, destructive confirm, back to first run)
     ├─ What if             later
     ├─ Accounts
     └─ Plan as of a date   later

Any item → Change (from when, effect) → Cover it

Spend  (Track and Link)
 ├─ Purchase → notes, links, receipts, group
 ├─ Add purchase
 ├─ Import a CSV
 ├─ Needs a home
 ├─ Card reserve
 └─ Check a balance
```

Horizon sits after Goals and before Cards, so the pills read the same at Plan and Track except for the one Track adds at the end. The thin pill row is one line that scrolls sideways at every phone width (`PillRow isThin isScrolling`, drawn by `PlanScreen`); below 640px the pills drop their emoji mark so more of them show, a row that overflows fades at the edge it hides (so a pill past it peeks), and a selected pill is scrolled clear of the fade.

Home, Days and Plan are always present. Everything not a tab is a full screen or a sheet over the screen it came from.

## 5. User Journeys

Arrows read left to right. A branch is in parentheses.

1. **First session.** (Sign in, or start signed out and sign in at the end) → (How do you want to start? → Start from your budget → pick files or paste text → Read my budget → review what was found, open or remove any one → Add them to my plan, then the step after the last kind they filled, with those in them) or (Start fresh) → add an income source (schedule, amount, next date) → add a bill → add an envelope → add a goal → say where things stand (or "Later") → Home with a draft for the next income day. Only the income source is required. Coming back part-way asks "Continue where you left off" or "Start over".
2. **Payday.** Home → next income day → read the draft → (change a line for this day) → Confirm money arrived → enter amount and date → Confirmed → checklist → tick bills and transfers → Done.
3. **The money was not what was planned.** Confirm money arrived → enter the real amount → (more: a success notice, the extra adds to Free to Spend) or (less: a warning notice; covered by what is left from the day, or "Cover it from Free to Spend" with an Undo, then "What gets less" for any rest, Wants, then Shoulds, then Musts, with "Use these" or each item changed by hand) → add a note → Confirm → the day keeps planned and confirmed. Later: Correct this day → new amounts → (short beyond the day's cover: the same warning, cover and "What gets less" as Confirm) → the original stays beside the correction.
4. **Change the plan.** Plan → open Groceries → Change → new amount → from when → effect on each upcoming day → (a day is short: Top up or Cover it, pick what gives) → Apply change → change log entry with an optional note.
5. **Add a purchase** (Track and Link). Envelope or Spend → Add → "How much was it?" (the large figure, keyboard up) → Next → "What was it for?" (one of the five used most, or More; skipped when opened from an envelope; tapping one moves on by itself) → the folded answers with the day (today) → Add → (over the envelope: it shows how far over and Free to Spend drops; Cover it is not built yet).
6. **Link an account** (leaves the app). Settings → Tracking and linking → Link, or a card's or account's sheet → "Link to your bank", or Add a card → "Link it from your bank" → explainer (read-only, sign in at the bank) → the bank's own sign-in in a new tab → (not approved: back in Runway, nothing linked) → **Is this one of yours?** (a row per account the bank sent, each set to a guess: one of yours, Add as new, or Don't bring in; the card or account the person started from is already picked) → pick a start date → (a balance differs: "Use the bank's?") → Where is your money now? → hand-entered purchases offered as matches → Linked.
7. **Move down a level.** Settings → Tracking and linking → (unlink an account: new purchases stop, history stays) or (stop tracking: purchase screens hide, history stays) → figures change from _safe to spend_ to _left_, or to _assigned_ → recorded in the change log.
8. **An expected expense or income.** (As built the form saves the bill or income at once; the proposal step is later.) Days → Add expected money → expense or income → name, amount or range, start date, repeats (and ends, if it repeats) → proposed changes for each income day it touches, with the total and the last date → (Edit) / Apply / Not now → applied changes appear in the change log as ordinary changes.
9. **The owner plans the move (Track).** Plan → Horizon → "Add to your horizon" → "Moving" + 📦 → Around Jan 15th → Add a cost ×3 (Movers $600 to $800 once Jan 15th; Truck rental $120 once Jan 14th, How it's paid → Paid outside the plan; Storage unit $100 monthly from Feb 1st) → Put money toward it now? $800 from Free to Spend → Where does the rest come from? "My pays before each cost is due" → review (Jan 15th, Jan 31st and the pays after them each put $50 toward Storage) → Add Moving → Moving's sheet: $800 left, "Covers Movers on Jan 15th. From Feb 1st, Storage comes from your pays."
10. **The quote comes in (Dec 2nd).** Moving → Movers → Set the price → An exact amount → $600 → What changes (4 pays put $50 less) → Why: Got a quote → Set the price → Moving: "Covers Movers, then Storage on Feb 1st and Mar 1st. From Apr 1st, Storage comes from your pays."
11. **The truck (Jan 14th).** Moving → Truck rental ("Jan 14th · outside the plan") → "Mark paid" ("You pay it yourself, so nothing comes from Moving.") → paid at once, toast with Undo; Moving's left is unchanged.
12. **Paying the movers (Jan 15th, Track).** Home's "To pay today" → Movers · Moving → the cost sheet's footer "Add what you paid" (purchase form; Movers · Moving and Amount $600.00 folded) → What did you pay with? Venture X → Add → Moving: $200 left. (Second way: the cost sheet's "Mark paid" row → paid at once, toast with Undo, no purchase.) (Plan: the same row → "Mark paid" → paid at once.) (From the Jan 15th checklist: tick "Movers · Moving" → paid at once.)
13. **Paying a range (Dec 18th, Plan).** Sam's wedding → Flight → "Mark paid" → What did Flight come to? (empty, "It varies, usually $350 to $450") → $400 → notice "$500.00 → $100.00" and "Hotel on Dec 19th is then $180.00 short" → Mark paid → Sam's wedding: "$180 short on Dec 19th".
14. **Taking a payment back.** Movers → "Paid Jan 15th · $600.00" → Take it back → Moving $200 → $800, Movers unpaid again. (Track with a purchase: the same tap also removes the purchase from Spend, and the confirm says so.) (On a done item: the item reopens.)
15. **Storage becomes a bill (Jan 16th).** Moving's footer "Make Storage unit a bill" → Bring Moving's $200 with it? Yes (What changes: Mar 15th and Mar 31st's $50 moves to Storage unit) → Make it a bill → Bills lists Storage unit, holding $200 → Moving: "Everything is paid or a bill", footer "Finish Moving" → $0 left, so one tap → toast with Undo → Done · 3 (with the couch and the wedding). (No, make it Free to Spend → Free to Spend +$200, Jan 31st puts $100 toward Storage unit.)
16. **The move is off (branch, Dec 2nd).** Moving → Call it off → What stops (Movers, Storage unit) → What changes (Mar 15th, Mar 31st: $50 → $0) → What happens to the $800 left? Make it Free to Spend (or Keep it held, or Move it to an envelope or goal → pick) → Why? The plans changed → Call it off → Moving under Done, "Called off Dec 2nd".
17. **The move is back on (branch, Dec 20th).** Done → Moving ("Called off Dec 2nd") → Bring it back → What comes back (Movers, Storage unit) → What changes (the pays from Jan 15th put $50 toward Storage again, more where it starts at $0) → Bring it back → Bring the $800 back to Moving? (Bring the $800 back, or Start Moving at $0) → What changes for that answer → toast with Undo, Moving open again.
18. **The couch is done (Oct 20th).** New couch → footer "Finish New couch" → What happens to the $20 left? Make it Free to Spend → Finish New couch → Done, "$20 to Free to Spend, Oct 20th".
19. **A wedding with a gap.** Plan → Horizon → Sam's wedding ($230 short on Dec 19th) → the warning's "Take it from my pays" (the pays before Dec 19th put in $230 between them, the effect shown first) or "Put more toward it" (amount, from where) → the shortfall goes.
20. **The brother's surgery (Plan).** Plan → Horizon → Add to your horizon → Miso's surgery → Surgery $450 to $600 once Dec 18th → Put money toward it now? Not now → "My pays before each cost is due" → each pay before Dec 18th shows "Surgery · Miso's surgery · due Dec 18th" → the Dec 11th pay's checklist → tick Surgery → What did Surgery come to? $520 → $80 left stays with the item. No purchase or card is ever on screen.
21. **A pay's line opens its item.** Days → Fri, Jan 15th → Storage unit · Moving (due Feb 1st, $50) → its sheet → "Open Moving" opens the item over the day, and Back returns to the day.

Notes on the table:

- "Amount" is always `AmountField` drawn as the hero figure (`isHero`): exact by default, "It varies" under it for a range. A range shows both ends and the plan's stance ("Planned at the low end"), and "Plan it at one end" opens the stance pills.
- _As built (redesign):_ every create form is a `QuestionFlow`. Bill: name, how much, when it is due (no day picked to start; picking one sets Repeats to Monthly, Once for an expected expense) and whether it repeats, then folded: ends (never), paid from (spread over income days), priority (Should), emoji (none). Envelope: name, how much each time (with "Every income day ›" under the figure), then folded: funded, priority, emoji. Goal: name, target, by when (empty for no date), how much each time (prefilled from the target and the date, with the pace it gives), then folded: funded, priority, emoji. Income: where it comes from, take-home pay, how often and the next payday, then folded: ends, what the pay is called (Paycheck). The pinned footer's primary is Next until every answer has been seen, then Add. Purchase: how much, what it was for, then folded: the day (today), with "Add a place" and "Add a note" chips. _As built (round 3):_ a question answered by one pick (a purchase's envelope, priority, how often an envelope or goal gets money, a bill's or pay's ending, a bill's repeat once its date is in, an income source) moves on as it is picked; a pick that reveals more to fill (Chosen days, a date ending) waits for Next. A field a chip reveals takes focus. Cancel or Discard on a form opened from an item's sheet returns to that sheet. "Where things stand" asks Keep editing / Discard like the other forms. A change opened at its amount has no "Every income day ›" link under the figure, since its Funded row is right below. A change opened from an income day shows "From when" open with "This income day only" and "From this income day on" first; elsewhere it is folded to "From now on ›". An envelope's change says which pairing sets it on its income days and no longer sends the person to another tab. _As built (round 4, Starting out):_ income asks "How much lands?" with "How often it arrives comes next" under the figure; "It varies" splits it and "The same each time" takes it back; "Choose which end to plan for" asks one question, "Which end should the plan count on?", with the end the plan already uses marked "like your plan" and a help icon saying what planning at one end does. Twice a month asks two days of the month as selects (1st to 30th and "Last day"), not a date. A bill's name has quick picks (Rent, Mortgage, Electricity, Water, Internet, Phone, Car payment, Car insurance, Health insurance, Streaming, Gym) that fill the name and an emoji, and an envelope's has Groceries, Dining out, Gas, Household and Fun money.
- "Priority" is Must, Should or Want on a bill, envelope or goal. "Funding rule" is when an envelope receives money. They are different fields and never share a row.
- No entity has an initial set long enough to need steps or `Disclosure`, apart from the later tax and deduction list.
- Priority is asked on the form of a bill, envelope or goal, and starts at Should so a quick add never has to answer it.
- Names: "Add to your horizon" makes an item; "Add a cost" adds a cost; neither is called "New". The item never calls its costs "lines" and a pay never calls Moving's figure a "draft" figure (rules, "Speak in the person's nouns").

## Horizon canvas

The boards for Plan > 🗓️ Horizon are the "Runway UX: Horizon" artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/8NrXzdaLvjj9B4GAMDSXiN), 390 x 844 phone boards in Runway's own tokens (nothing new). Row A plans it, row B prices and pays it, row C is payday, row D ends it.

- **Board 1, Plan > Horizon (interactive):** the list, the item and the cost sheet, with Tweaks for the level (Plan or Track), the moment (Nov 3rd, Dec 2nd, Jan 16th), hidden amounts and the theme.
- **Boards 2 to 4:** Add to your horizon and what it doesn't cover; Add a cost, a range; Moving on Nov 3rd while planning.
- **Boards 5, 6, 6b and 8:** Set the price (Dec 2nd); Movers on Jan 15th at Track, two ways to pay an exact cost; Flight on Dec 18th at Plan, where a range asks what it came to; Storage becomes a bill (Jan 16th).
- **Board 7:** Fri, Jan 15th, the money arrived: what a pay puts toward Horizon and what it pays.
- **Board 9:** Call off Moving (a branch on Dec 2nd).

Each board carries one filled primary at most and every figure matches the example data below; Plan boards say "paid", never "spent", "purchase" or a card, and hidden amounts leave no "short" word and no warning colour.

**Example data, used on every board and here.** Today is Tue, Nov 3rd, 2026, conservative stance (a range is planned at its high end). The owner (Track) has one Work pay, $2,140.00, on the 15th and the last day of each month. **📦 Moving**, around Jan 15th: Truck rental $120 on Thu, Jan 14th, paid outside the plan (work covers it; marked paid on Jan 14th); Movers $600 to $800 on Fri, Jan 15th; Storage unit $100 a month from Mon, Feb 1st, no end yet. $800 set aside from Free to Spend on Nov 3rd. The rest comes from his pays. **💍 Sam's wedding**, Dec 18th to 19th: Flight $350 to $450 on Dec 18th, Hotel $280 on Dec 19th, $500 set aside, the rest "I'll decide later". **🛋️ New couch**, done Oct 20th, $1,180 spent.

Three moments reconcile across boards:

| Moment | Moving: left | Set aside / spent | What it covers | What the pays carry for Moving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 3rd, planning | $800 | $800 / $0 | Movers on Jan 15th (at $800) | Storage from Feb 1st: $50 on each pay from Jan 15th on |
| Dec 2nd, price set at $600 | $800 | $800 / $0 | Movers, then Storage on Feb 1st and Mar 1st | Storage from Apr 1st: $50 on Mar 15th and Mar 31st |
| Jan 16th, movers paid | $200 | $800 / $600 | Storage on Feb 1st and Mar 1st | the same |
| Jan 16th, Storage made a bill, "Yes" | $0 (the bill holds $200) | $800 / $600, $200 moved to the bill | nothing; Moving can finish | the bill: $50 on Mar 15th and Mar 31st for Apr 1st |
| Jan 16th, Storage made a bill, "No" | $0 ($200 to Free to Spend) | the same | nothing | the bill: $100 on Jan 31st for Feb 1st, then $50 on each pay |

Sam's wedding at the high end: Flight $450 leaves $50, so the Hotel on Dec 19th is $230 short. Dec 18th, Flight marked paid at $400 (Plan): $100 left, the Hotel $180 short.

Two branches, drawn on board 9 and nowhere else: **Moving called off on Dec 2nd** (after the price is set): $800 left, nothing paid; Movers and Storage stop, so Mar 15th and Mar 31st no longer put $50 toward Storage. **New couch finished on Oct 20th**: $1,200 set aside, $1,180 spent, $20 left, made Free to Spend (the done item's "$20 to Free to Spend, Oct 20th").

A cost's line name is the cost, then the event, on every surface: "Movers · Moving".

## Round 4, package C: Home, Days, Plan and Spend

- **Warnings.** The problems row on Home and Days, the rows it opens and a day's "The plan leaves Rent $30 short" line use the warning tint, edge and icon (`--runway-warning-*` in `runway.css`), never the app's blue.
- **Bills to pay.** A bill due today or past due and not marked paid is a card, not a problem: "To pay today" ("To pay now" when one is late) sits right under Free to Spend on Home and at the top of Bills, one row per bill (badge, name, "Due today", "Was due yesterday" or, for a bill missed more than once, "Missed Oct 1st and Nov 1st" / "Missed Oct 1st and 3 more" / "Missed Oct 1st · due again today", oldest first and by name on a tie, the amount masked) with a quiet Mark paid that records it and offers an Undo toast; the payment also appears in the bill's History with a way to take it back, the same as Mark paid in the bill's sheet. A bill whose amount is a range is never marked paid at its planned figure: Mark paid (sheet, card or checklist tick) opens "What did Electric come to?" with the amount empty and "It varies, usually $100 to $140" under it, says what more than was set aside comes out of Free to Spend, what stays set aside, or which card it is charged to, and on the payday checklist such a bill reads "Varies · tick to say what it came to", which "Mark all paid" leaves to be ticked, saying so. Confirm money arrived does the same for pay that varies: the amount starts empty with the range as its hint. With many bills the header says how many ("21 bills, oldest first"); Home shows three with "See all" on the header line, which opens Bills with the card unfolded. Bills draws the card under a caption-sized heading (a section inside Bills) with three rows and a "Show 18 more" row, a "Show fewer" on the heading's line once unfolded, then an "All bills" divider before the list. A paycheck's plan shows a warning door under its hero card when bills are missing from it, said in terms of this pay ("Rent has no due date" over "So this pay sets nothing aside for it, and has less to spare than it looks"; "Gym and Health insurance have no due date"), which opens the bill's due question or Bills narrowed to the undated bills (a chip clears it).
- **Things to fix.** One row per problem, soonest first: "Rent $30 short" over "On Nov 2nd", an icon chip and a chevron; a bill with no income reads "Due Nov 5th" ("Due tomorrow" the day before). The counted row above Free to Spend names the first of the same list and counts the rest. The list is open while `?fixes=true` is in the URL, so Back, Escape and the header's back from a row's income day land on the open list, and one more Back closes it. The engine reports the first short line of each income day, so a day with several short lines shows one row.
- **Screens opened from an income day.** Check an amount sits above the day (Back returns to it); an item's History and Purchases are views inside its sheet, so the day stays where it was.
- **Days.** The headline, its help icon and "Add expected" share one row; below 390px the action is a calendar icon named "Add expected money".
- **Plan.** Income, Bills, Envelopes and Goals are the four view pills, each with an emoji (dropped below 360px). **What it's for (Issue 39, extended to envelopes in #561 and to goals):** bills on Bills, envelopes on Envelopes and goals on Goals sit under one `SectionDivider`-style heading per name (`GroupHeading`: a button that opens its `GroupSheet`) with "$140 to $180 a month" under it, of that list's kind (Bills counts a purpose's bills, Envelopes its envelopes, Goals instead says what its goals have saved of their targets, "$0 of $2,000 saved", since its rows are balances; a kind whose members have no set monthly amount (a percent of pay) reads "No set amount a month", never "$0.00"; `useGroupFigures`); when the purpose has other kinds with a regular monthly figure it reads that kind's share of the purpose's total ("$835 of $925 a month" on Bills, "$90 of $925 a month" on Envelopes, each amount its own mask, "•••• of •••• a month" while hidden), so a heading never disagrees with the purpose's total; the sheet's captions keep "Bills · $40.00 a month" under its combined hero, the purpose's emoji before its name when it has one, the amount one mask while hidden, and a help icon after that line that says what the total counts ("Bills come to $835 of Car's $925 a month. Tap Car to see everything in it." when the purpose has other kinds), with the range sentence when it is a range; one with only one-time bills leads with them ("$300.00 due Nov 20th") and an empty one says "Nothing here yet". Unset rows follow under "Other bills" (Bills), "Other envelopes" (Envelopes) or "Other goals" (Goals) once any name exists, and a name that was stopped lists its rows there. Search filters across sections. The sheet's head is the combined "Together, each month" lead and help, then the bills, envelopes and goals it is for as short lists (a goal's row says what it comes to a month like an envelope's; a paused goal says so and is not counted), each under a caption with its own monthly total ("Bills · $40.00 a month", masked), a Priority pill row per section it has (Bills, Envelopes, Goals, and Everything above them when it has two or more, reading Mixed when they differ; Must, Should or Want first confirms, naming each item that changes, then moves every item already for the name to that priority in one write, an envelope's or goal's extras folded into its amount; None changes only what a new one starts with), "Add a bill for Pets", "Add an envelope for Pets", "Add a goal for Pets", "Choose an emoji for Pets" ("Change Pets's emoji" once set, with Remove emoji; a bill, envelope or goal with no emoji of its own wears it on every row, else its first letter), "Rename Pets" (refused when another has that name: "You already have Pets.") and "Stop using Pets" (confirm: "Its 3 bills, 2 envelopes and 1 goal stay in your plan, with nothing set."). A bill's, envelope's or goal's sheet lists "For" below History and stays open on its menu after a move. When nothing is set yet and a list has 3 rows or more, one muted tip line under it says what it is for. Settings and tools (Settings, Change log) is a tile under the section, not a pill.
- **Spend.** A thin chip on the "Purchases" title line ("This month ⌄", `ScopeChip`: 28px with a 40px hit area, a drawer on a phone, a popover from `sm`) picks the period: quick periods, then older months that can hold purchases; "Spent" sits under it as the figure for the rows shown; `?period=` keeps it.
- **Calls to action** carry no "+": "Add income", "Add a bill", "Add an envelope", "Add a goal", "Add a purchase", "Add expected".
- **See all** sits on Home's Envelopes header line; "Show N more" on Days is a 14px muted row.

## Round 4, package D: private amounts

- **Priority dial.** On Plan's Bills, Envelopes and Goals views (once the view lists something) the title line holds a small Must, Should, Want dial before the eye: a rounded track about 112px wide with three round notches like a wizard's steps, each named in small type under it (`NotchedSlider`), so it never reads as a second row of tabs. The knob follows a finger and snaps to the nearest notch on release; a tap on a notch or its name, or the arrow keys, move it too. It starts on Want, which counts everything; Must or Should trims the view's header total and its group headings to those priorities ("$1,400.00 a month, Musts only", "$350.00 saved without Wants"), haloes the dial's knob, dims the badge and figure of each row it leaves out (their text stays readable, and a screen reader hears "Not in this total") and adds one line to the total's help icon saying what is counted and that it goes back to Want. The setting holds across the three views while Runway is open and goes back to everything when the person leaves Runway, so a trimmed total never outlives the moment it was asked for. A long trimmed total wraps to a second line rather than running into the title.
- **Priority extras.** The envelope and goal forms ask one more folded question right after the priority, "Should it get more when there's room?" (summary row "Extra when there's room · None" or "+$250 want"), with an amount field for each level below the item's own ("As a Should", "As a Want") and one hint line; it shows only for an exact amount on a schedule and a priority above Want. A split item's row reads the total its parts ask ($200 for $150 Must +$50 Want) over "Every income day", adding "+$50 want" only while the dial leaves that part out, and its sheet's fact line spells out the split ("Must · $150 must, +$50 want"); an income day lists each part as its own line ("Groceries · must", "Groceries · want"), and Priorities draws one row per part in its tier, where a part can move only between its neighbours' levels (no Move up or place slot past them).
- **Priorities nudge.** While the plan has three or more bills, envelopes and goals and every one is still Should (the default), Home shows one quiet row under Free to Spend, "Mark what you must pay" ("Everything starts as Should. Move yours into Must and Want."), that opens Priorities. It goes away once any priority changes, or for good on this device when its worded "Don't ask again" is tapped (never a bare X, which reads as "not now"). Home asks one thing at a time: while anything else on Home asks (the warning row, things to fix such as bills with no due date, or "To pay now"), the nudge waits until nothing does, and the Track invite takes the same quiet slot: it shows only when nothing else asks and the nudge doesn't apply.
- The eye is drawn only where something masked is on the surface (a sheet, form or subview gets it once a masked figure is in it, such as a folded answer or an effect preview). Each tab's title line ends in one eye button (Home, Days, Plan, Spend, same place); it shows or hides amounts on every tab. Amounts start hidden on a device, and hide again after five minutes in the background.
- Masked: every figure on every surface (tab fronts, item sheets, a goal's "of $1,800", the day screens and their rows, Things to fix, the envelope sheet, Check an amount's result, Purchases, the change log and the record of a confirmed day), and every line that gives one away. Names, dates and counts stay readable. Never masked: only the field being typed in (the open question's hero). A form's folded answers, its notices and its footer reasons are masked like everything else. Every sheet, screen or subview that draws a masked figure has the eye on its title line.
- A goal's sheet offers "Change how often" (while it is being added to) or "Start adding to it" (when it is not); both open the goal's "How often" question. A calendar envelope's sheet labels its figure by its cadence ("Monthly").
- "Things to fix" lists every short line of an income day as its own row, soonest first, the higher priority first within a day.

## Round 4, package G4: floating adds, Free to Spend, filters, warnings, goal chart

- **Every tab's add action floats.** One pill at the bottom right above the tab bar (`FloatingAction`, an icon and a label): Home and Spend "Add a purchase" (Home at Track only), Days "Add expected money", Plan the view's own ("Add a bill", "Add a goal", "Add an envelope", "Add income"). It hides while a sheet, drawer or subview is open and drops to the icon while scrolling down; the label is always its accessible name. The header lines that held the old buttons are now free: Days' headline line takes the full width, and nothing else was put in them.
- **Free to Spend is a worked sum.** Free to Spend, plus left in envelopes, plus held for goals, plus held for bills (and, when non-zero, plus set aside for cards), equals everything you have (the money on hand), one operand per row with the signs in their own column; each held row opens its Plan view. Amounts are masked while hidden.
- **"Change what you started with" is a correction window.** Offered until the first income day from the start is confirmed and for at most 14 days after the start record was made; afterwards one quiet line says to correct the income day or bill payment.
- **Spend's envelope filter** is one icon beside the search (a drawer on a phone, a popover from `sm`); an active filter tints it and shows one chip that clears it. The search and icon show from the fifth purchase or when two envelopes have purchases.

## Group due date

A group's sheet has "Set one due date" ("For all N bills in this group") when it holds bills. It swaps in "When are they all due?" with one date control and "Set the due date". Each bill keeps its own repeat (a bill always has one, even undated). Paid one-time bills and bills ending before the day stay as they are, and the view says how many. When bills already have a due date, a confirm asks "Replace them with Nov 15th?" (names up to three). Each bill is written as its own change from today with its own change-log line; a partial failure names the bills that did not save.

The success toast carries one Undo that puts every written bill back as it was (its later scheduled changes included); the confirm names each bill with its current date, and the primary's footer says "Pick a day to continue" until a day is chosen.
