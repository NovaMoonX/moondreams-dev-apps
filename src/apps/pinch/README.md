# Pinch 🧂

Pinch is a calm, personal kitchen planner. It comes stocked with everyday ingredients and seasonings, keeps your recipes and the meals you build from them, and puts what you plan to eat on a calendar. Add what you pay for things and every recipe, meal and week knows its cost. Nutrition is there when you want it. Planning meals in an AI chat starts from scratch each time and cannot see your kitchen or prices, and Pinch keeps that context.

## How it works

1. Start with the ingredient catalog, add your own, and attach stores, prices and package sizes.
2. Add recipes by hand, or from pasted text, a photo, a PDF or a web link, and review what the AI fills in.
3. Put meals together from recipes and ingredients, and plan them on the calendar.
4. Make a shopping list from a recipe, a meal or a whole day, and tick things off at the store.
5. Cook step by step with timers and voice commands, then mark the recipe cooked.
6. Keep leftovers in the fridge or freezer and track your pantry.
