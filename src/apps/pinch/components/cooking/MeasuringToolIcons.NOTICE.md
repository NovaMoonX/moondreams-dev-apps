# Third-party icons

Three of the measuring tool icons come from the Hugeicons free stroke-rounded set, as published on npm in `@iconify-json/hugeicons` 1.2.36 (the Iconify API was not reachable when they were taken). The dry measuring cup is drawn for Pinch in the same style.

| `LiquidCupIcon`    | `hugeicons:beaker`          | Hugeicons | MIT     | [Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com) |
| `SpoonsIcon`       | `hugeicons:spoon`           | Hugeicons | MIT     | [Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com) |
